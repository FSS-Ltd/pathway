# Privacy complaints procedure

**Contact:** privacy@faithfulsoftware.dev. The responsible operator is Faithful Software Solutions Ltd. The owner confirmed on 15 October 2026 that they monitor this mailbox. A complaint register, backup owner and response tracking still need to be established.

1. Record the receipt date, complainant's safe contact method, the issue and whether it concerns data we control or an organisation controls. Keep records access-restricted and avoid copying unnecessary child or health details into the complaint log.
2. Acknowledge receipt within 30 days. Explain the next step and request only the information needed to locate the case or verify identity and authority.
3. Without undue delay, investigate, preserve relevant evidence, involve the correct controller, and update the complainant on progress. Route safeguarding or security incidents into their established incident process promptly; do not wait for the complaint deadline.
4. Explain the outcome and any action without undue delay. Tell the person how to contact the ICO if dissatisfied. Record the response date and action taken.
5. Review complaint themes and update the relevant product, notice or process when needed. Retain complaint records under an approved schedule, then securely delete them.

This procedure describes the required operation. It does not assert that an inbox automation, case-management system or approved retention period already exists. The operator must establish response tracking and verify the arrangements as part of the publication check.
