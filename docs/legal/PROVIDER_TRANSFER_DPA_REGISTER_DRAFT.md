# Nexsteps provider, transfer and DPA register — verification draft

**Owner:** Faithful Software Solutions Ltd

**Prepared:** 15 October 2026

**Status:** Working inventory. A public vendor DPA link is **not** proof that FSS has accepted that DPA, enabled a particular region, or completed a UK transfer assessment.

The [ICO's controller/processor contract guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/contracts-and-liabilities-between-controllers-and-processors-multi/what-needs-to-be-included-in-the-contract) describes the terms needed when a processor is engaged. Its [international transfer guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/) requires checking whether each actual flow is restricted, then recording any adequacy route or appropriate safeguard and, where required, a data protection test (also called a transfer risk assessment). A selected hosting region does not prove that support, telemetry or subprocessors stay there.

## Evidence to obtain, provider by provider

1. Identify the FSS-owned account/tenant and production project. Capture only account/project identifiers safe for internal documentation; keep credentials out of Git.
2. Save the effective DPA or contractual terms, acceptance date, party names and subprocessor notice subscription in an access-controlled legal folder. Record a reference here, not a signed contract or private account export.
3. Map each data category and flow, including support/admin access, telemetry, backup and subprocessors. Verify actual countries from the live settings and vendor support record.
4. For each UK-to-overseas restricted transfer, record the exact adequacy regulation or safeguard. If using an IDTA or UK Addendum, complete the [ICO data protection test](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/completing-a-transfer-risk-assessment/) where required. Do not assume a vendor's generic DPA completes this FSS-specific step.
5. Record provider deletion/return behaviour and retention, then compare it with the [retention schedule](./RETENTION_SCHEDULE_DRAFT.md) and the public notice. Assign an owner and annual or change-triggered review date.
