## Repository and service evidence

- The public site has a privacy page, business terms and a cookie policy. The previous privacy page used the page-render date as “Last updated”, which was not an effective date.
- Parent registration currently requires a `dataProcessingConsent` boolean while storing separate first-aid and photo choices. That historical field and its records must not be relabelled as agreement acceptance.
- Production Vercel project listings show `nexsteps-web`, `nexsteps-admin` and `nexsteps-api`. The API project lists environment variable _names_ for Clerk, Supabase, Stripe and Resend. No secret values are included in this record.
- The connected Supabase account lists an active `FSS` project in `eu-west-3`. An older deployment guide names a different Nexsteps project in `eu-west-1`. We have not established which project holds current production data. The policy therefore makes no specific database-region promise.
- The owner confirmed on 15 October 2026 that they monitor `privacy@faithfulsoftware.dev`. They also confirmed that an approved retention schedule and provider transfer/DPA records do not yet exist.
- The worker defines configurable retention periods for attendance, staff activity and audit events, but retention execution depends on `RETENTION_ENABLED`. The Home deletion endpoint files a support-routed request. Neither proves a production deletion time or a complete retention schedule.

## Publication checks

Before treating these drafts as a verified public notice, the owner must confirm:

1. Establish a complaint register and ownership arrangement that supports the confirmed monitored mailbox, 30-day acknowledgement and ongoing response duties.
2. Complete and approve the [provider transfer/DPA register](./PROVIDER_TRANSFER_DPA_REGISTER_DRAFT.md), including production locations and international access countries, the applicable adequacy route or transfer instrument, and where a person can obtain a copy.
3. Complete and approve the [retention/deletion schedule](./RETENTION_SCHEDULE_DRAFT.md) for accounts, household records, child records, logs, invoices, backups and acceptance evidence, including whether retention jobs actually run in production.
4. The purpose-level controller/processor and lawful-basis mapping for direct household child records, including any legitimate-interest assessment and Article 9 condition when special-category information can be entered.
5. That each organisation supplies its own child/parent privacy information, Article 6 basis, Article 9 condition where needed, and photo-use withdrawal route.

These are factual and legal publication checks, not settings the engineering team should guess. A draft PR can carry the text for review, but it does not clear these checks or authorise a production release.
