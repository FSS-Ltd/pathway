# Nexsteps retention schedule — approval draft

**Owner:** Faithful Software Solutions Ltd

**Prepared:** 15 October 2026

**Status:** Draft; no periods or deletion controls below are approved as a company-wide policy.

This is a decision register, not evidence that deletion already runs. The [ICO's storage-limitation guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/storage-limitation/) calls for documented periods or criteria, review when the period ends, and erasure or anonymisation unless continued retention has a clear reason. The controller must also pause ordinary deletion when a specific legal, safeguarding or dispute hold applies, record the reason, and review the hold.

## Approval and implementation steps

1. The owner identifies every production database, storage bucket, identity tenant, log sink and backup service, then confirms the data classes each holds.
2. For organisation-controlled records, obtain documented retention instructions from each controller. For Home and FSS-controlled records, approve the period or criteria, trigger, legal basis, exceptions and reviewer. Seek specialist review for safeguarding and special-category records.
3. Reconcile the proposed rules with actual worker configuration, scheduled execution and deletion paths. `RETENTION_ENABLED` and the worker's three defaults alone do not cover all records.
4. Test deletion and export on disposable data, including files, provider copies, backups and a legal hold. Record run date, counts, exceptions and reviewer. Never perform a production deletion as part of this draft.
5. Sign off the schedule with an approval date and owner, then update the public privacy wording to match what actually runs. Review at least annually and after provider or product changes.
