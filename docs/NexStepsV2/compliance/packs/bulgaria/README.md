# Bulgaria national checklist pack (2.08)

Reviewed 2026-10-18. Catalogue 1.9.0 adds **15 conditional actions** and **18 official/provider sources** across six institutional sectors and eight topics. It publishes a partial national and EU core; all 48 Bulgaria cells remain `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED` with regional status `IN_REVIEW`. Manual checklist completion is not legal certification.

The [Ministry of Education's current private-school and kindergarten register service](https://www.mon.bg/mon/administrativno-obsluzhvane/administrativni-uslugi-i-obraztsi-na-zayavleniya/) and [institution register](https://ri.mon.bg/) are separate from [Ministry of Health nursery rules](https://www.mh.government.bg/upload/4501/naredba26-ot-2008g-deinost-iasli-detski-kuhni.pdf). The pack asks every site to classify actual provision. Only an actual independent school receives the private-school registration action. An ACE or learning-centre screen set does not establish legal school status. Childcare routes depend on ages and service, not sector label alone.

The [State Agency for Child Protection](https://sacp.government.bg/signal) says a person who learns that a child needs protection must notify social assistance, the agency or police immediately. For employers, [workplace risk guidance](https://www.mlsp.government.bg/otsenka-na-riska-na-rabotnoto-myasto) and the [workplace safety law](https://www.mlsp.government.bg/uploads/37/politiki/obshchestveno-osiguryavane/zakon-za-zdravoslovni-i-bezopasni-usloviq-na-trud.pdf) support risk, first-aid, fire and evacuation actions. [EU GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) applies to actual processing roles; the [2026 ministry copy of the Food Act](https://www.mzh.government.bg/media/filer_public/2026/02/16/zakon_za_hranite.pdf) and [EU food hygiene rules](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32004R0852) are conditional on food activity. ICCE terms apply only to actual qualification providers.

The [oblast and municipal inventory](regional-review.md) lists unresolved local rules. Staff qualifications, nursery operating details, premises use, school programmes, transport and food-specific permissions need specialist review. Site managers should confirm uncertain applicability with the relevant local authority before marking an action not applicable. No automatic checkbox completion, UI change or database migration is introduced.
