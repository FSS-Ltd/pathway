# Bulgarian oblast and municipality rules still in review

| Boundary                            | Reviewed authority signpost                                                                                                             | Still required before a complete local pack                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 28 oblasts                          | [Regional Education Offices](https://www.mon.bg/mon/ministerstvo/regionalni-upravleniya-na-obrazovanieto/)                              | Validate school classification, approval changes, inspections, attendance and regional education procedures.                             |
| 28 oblasts                          | [Regional Health Inspectorates](https://www.mh.government.bg/bg/ministerstvo/vtorostepenni-razporediteli/regionalni-zdravni-inspektsii) | Validate nursery, public-health, medical and premises conditions by service and building.                                                |
| Regional food-safety authorities    | [Bulgarian Food Safety Agency register](https://public-iisr.bfsa.bg/BABHRegsExt/index.xhtml?faces-redirect=true)                        | Confirm food-activity classification, establishment registration, specialist approval and inspection.                                    |
| Municipalities and fire authorities | [Ministry of Labour workplace risk guidance](https://www.mlsp.government.bg/otsenka-na-riska-na-rabotnoto-myasto)                       | Verify building use, local fire, accessibility, transport and municipal permissions; national workplace rules alone do not resolve them. |

The [government administration register](https://iisda.government.bg/ras/adm_structures/district_administrations) lists 28 oblast administrations. Actual applicable duties depend on the site's oblast, municipality, provider category and activities. No reviewed oblast-specific checklist is published in this step; `IN_REVIEW` is not a finding of no obligations.
