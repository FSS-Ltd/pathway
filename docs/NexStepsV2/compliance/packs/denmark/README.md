# Denmark national checklist pack (2.12)

Reviewed 2026-10-19. Catalogue 1.13.0 adds **16 actions** and **17 official/provider sources** across six institutional sectors and eight topics. All 48 Denmark cells remain `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED` with regional status `IN_REVIEW`. Manual checklist completion is not legal certification.

[The education ministry](https://uvm.dk/grundskole/frie-grundskoler/om-frie-grundskoler/vejledning-til-oprettelse-af-en-fri-grundskole-eller-afdeling-heraf/) separates notification of a free primary school from approval for state subsidy. [Local school oversight](https://uvm.dk/grundskole/frie-grundskoler/tilsyn/skolens-lokale-tilsyn/om-selvevaluering/) uses approved self-evaluation or a certified supervisor. [Public administration guidance](https://lifeindenmark.borger.dk/family-and-children/childcare/rules-for-day-care-facilities) distinguishes private daycare facilities, independent facilities, family daycare and private childcare arrangements, with municipal approval or agreements. An ACE or Learning Centre screen set does not establish school status, childcare subtype or public funding.

[The social ministry](https://www.sm.dk/arbejdsomraader/boern-og-unge-i-udsatte-positioner/underretningspligt) explains general and professional child-welfare notification duties. [Police guidance](https://politi.dk/boerneattest) makes child-certificate collection role- and under-15-contact-specific; an adult account alone does not trigger it. [Danish data-protection guidance](https://www.datatilsynet.dk/regler-og-vejledning/skoler-og-daginstitutioner), [EU GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), [workplace APV guidance](https://at.dk/kend-din-rolle/arbejdsgiver/arbejdspladsvurdering/) and [food-authority registration guidance](https://foedevarestyrelsen.dk/kost-og-foedevarer/start-og-drift-af-foedevarevirksomhed/autorisation-og-registrering) support conditional actions. ICCE terms apply only where qualifications are actually offered.

The [regional and municipal inventory](regional-review.md) records 98 municipalities and a regional boundary change due in 2027. Municipal approval criteria, other school forms, staff checks, care ratios, premises, trips and specialist food routes require further authority review. Site managers should confirm uncertain applicability with the competent authority before marking a review action not applicable. No automatic checkbox completion, UI change or database migration is introduced.
