# Denmark municipal and regional rules still in review

[Statistics Denmark](https://www.dst.dk/da/Statistik/dokumentation/statistikdokumentation/kommunal--og-regionalvalg/sammenlignelighed) records 98 municipalities and five regions in 2026. Region Hovedstaden and Region Sjælland are scheduled to merge as Region Østdanmark on 2027-01-01, leaving four regions. Municipal authorities remain the key approval and supervision boundary for many childcare services. No municipality-specific checklist is published in this step; `IN_REVIEW` is not a finding of no obligations.

| Boundary             | Reviewed authority signpost                                                                                                                                          | Still required before complete local coverage                                                                               |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Municipal childcare  | [Private daycare rules](https://lifeindenmark.borger.dk/family-and-children/childcare/rules-for-day-care-facilities)                                                 | Verify each operating municipality's approval criteria, care subtype, capacity, inspection and parent information.          |
| Free primary schools | [Creation and subsidy guidance](https://uvm.dk/grundskole/frie-grundskoler/om-frie-grundskoler/vejledning-til-oprettelse-af-en-fri-grundskole-eller-afdeling-heraf/) | Confirm actual school category, state-aid status and ministry oversight route.                                              |
| Premises             | [Municipal daycare rules](https://lifeindenmark.borger.dk/family-and-children/childcare/rules-for-day-care-facilities)                                               | Confirm building use, fire, accessibility and hygiene authority for each location.                                          |
| Food                 | [Danish food authority](https://foedevarestyrelsen.dk/kost-og-foedevarer/start-og-drift-af-foedevarevirksomhed/autorisation-og-registrering)                         | Confirm activity scale, de minimis threshold and any specialist registration or approval.                                   |
| 2027 regions         | [Statistics Denmark regional change](https://www.dst.dk/da/Statistik/dokumentation/statistikdokumentation/kommunal--og-regionalvalg/sammenlignelighed)               | Reconcile region codes and authority contacts before and after 2027-01-01; do not silently apply a 2026 map to later sites. |

The operating municipality must be captured from the site, not inferred from billing address or timezone. The EU Denmark pack does not cover Greenland or the Faroe Islands, which the [EU lists outside its member-state territory](https://european-union.europa.eu/principles-countries-history/eu-countries/denmark_en). Their applicable local regimes need separate classification if a site operates there.
