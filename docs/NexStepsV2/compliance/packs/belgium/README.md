# Belgium shared-core checklist pack (2.07)

Reviewed 2026-10-18. Catalogue 1.8.0 publishes 12 conditional actions and 16 official/provider sources across six institutional sectors and eight topics. This is a **partial shared core**. The national Belgium cells remain `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`, with Community and regional status `IN_REVIEW`. Completion measures manual checklist confirmations, not legal certification.

Catalogue 1.38.0 adds three [geographic Region selectors](regional-review.md)
with 144 `IN_REVIEW` cells. The national cells remain
`PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`; a Region selection does not establish the competent
language Community or complete the local legal review.

Belgium's [language Communities govern education](https://www.belgium.be/en/education/coming_to_study_in_belgium). A screen set, ACE identity and funding status do not determine school recognition or childcare authorisation. The [Flemish distinction between recognised and unrecognised education](https://www.vlaanderen.be/onderwijs-en-vorming/kwaliteitszorg-in-het-onderwijs/erkende-en-niet-erkende-onderwijsinstellingen) is different from subsidy; family home-education duties for an unrecognised school are not turned into a universal provider duty. [Opgroeien](https://www.opgroeien.be/aanbod/kinderopvang/over-kinderopvang) and [ONE](https://my.one.be/information/milieux-accueil/) describe separate childcare authorisation routes. The German-speaking Community's [school recognition guidance](https://ostbelgienbildung.be/PortalData/21/Resources/downloads/home/schulvorschriften/Schulvorschrift_Gruendung_Schliessung.pdf) also separates recognition from subsidy.

The shared core uses [Federal Justice Model 596-2 guidance](https://www.justice.belgium.be/fr/themes_et_dossiers/documents/demander_des_documents/extrait_de_casier_judiciaire) for relevant child-facing roles, [Federal Employment workplace risk and fire guidance](https://emploi.belgique.be/fr/themes/bien-etre-au-travail/lieux-de-travail/prevention-incendie-sur-le-lieu-de-travail), [EU GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) with [Belgian DPA school guidance](https://www.gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be/professioneel/thema-s/onderwijs), and [FAVV/AFSCA food authorisation](https://www.foodweb.favv-afsca.be/professionnels/agrements/) where actual activities trigger them. ICCE requirements only apply to actual qualification providers.

The [Community and regional inventory](regional-review.md) identifies unresolved routes. Site managers should confirm applicability with the competent Community, Region or local authority before treating an item as not applicable. No automatic checkbox completion or new database migration is introduced.

Community-specific source records carry `BE` for the catalogue's parent-jurisdiction validation and a second Community identifier for their actual scope. The `BE` parent does not make a Flemish, French or German-speaking rule nationally applicable; the published actions only ask the site to establish its competent authority.
