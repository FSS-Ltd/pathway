# Belgian Community and regional rules still in review

Territorial inventory checked 21 October 2026 against [Eurostat's NUTS
correspondence table](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/nuts/correspondence-tables)
and the [Belgian federal description of the three
Regions](https://www.belgium.be/en/about_belgium/government/regions).
Catalogue 1.38.0 offers geographic Region selectors: `BE1`
Brussels-Capital, `BE2` Flemish, and `BE3` Walloon. All 144 new
Region/sector/topic cells are `IN_REVIEW`; they publish no regional legal
duties. The [federal description of three
Communities](https://www.belgium.be/en/about_belgium/government/communities)
confirms that the Flemish, French and German-speaking Communities are a
different authority dimension. Geographic selection alone cannot determine
the education or childcare regulator in Brussels or the German-speaking area.
The site profile now records separate education-Community and childcare-authority
declarations. Primary-source legal review remains incomplete for each Community,
Region and relevant municipality.

These are unresolved coverage boundaries, not a finding of no obligations. The site profile must identify the relevant language Community and physical Region; its current country/subdivision fields are insufficient to infer every Brussels service authority. The national checklist deliberately asks for an authority classification rather than applying one Community's rule to all Belgium sites.

## Site language-Community classification

The site profile now records the language Community responsible for the actual
education provision as a separate, nullable fact: Flemish, French, or German-speaking.
This follows the [Belgian federal distinction between
Communities](https://www.belgium.be/en/about_belgium/government/communities)
and [geographic Regions](https://www.belgium.be/en/about_belgium/government/regions),
rechecked 21 October 2026. The selection is an institutional declaration, not
an inference from the Region or evidence of registration. Childcare licensing is
a separate question: [Opgroeien's Brussels starter guidance](https://www.opgroeien.be/aanbod/kinderopvang/starters)
distinguishes Opgroeien, ONE and Iriscare routes for childcare. An unknown value
stays unresolved. The catalogue can condition future reviewed rules on this
fact, but no Community-specific legal duty is published by the profile change.

## Childcare authority and reviewed entry permissions

Reviewed 21 October 2026 against [Opgroeien's starter
route](https://www.opgroeien.be/aanbod/kinderopvang/starters), [ONE's
authorisation FAQ](https://my.one.be/information/faq/petite-enfance/),
[Iriscare's Brussels guidance](https://www.iriscare.brussels/fr/professionnels/familles-avec-enfants/accueil-denfants/)
and the [German-speaking Community family
portal](https://ostbelgienfamilie.be/desktopdefault.aspx/tabid-5887/).
Belgian site profiles now separately declare the care regulator (Opgroeien,
ONE, Iriscare or the German-speaking Community Ministry) and whether paid
professional under-three care occurs. Those facts are not inferred from the
school's education Community, sector, funding or Brussels address. A selected
Belgian Region limits incompatible regulator declarations. A missing Region,
regulator or paid-care answer leaves conditional licensing actions unresolved.
Changing the profile's operating jurisdiction clears the declared education
Community and childcare authority so a previous Region's classification cannot
silently carry over.

Catalogue 1.39.0 publishes four statutory entry/continuing-authorisation
actions, each limited by the declared service and competent territory. The
German-speaking Ministry action also covers declared school-age childcare;
the other three actions are limited to paid professional under-three care.
Existing national and all Belgian Region coverage cells remain partial or
`IN_REVIEW`. The following still need primary-source item review: childcare
service subtypes, staffing, qualifications, ratios, inspection, school-age
care outside the German-speaking route, Brussels individual-provider choices,
school recognition and local premises permissions. A portal checkbox is a
manual confirmation, not proof of an external licence. Operators should
confirm applicability with the competent authority before treating a care
action as inapplicable.

## Flemish school recognition boundary

Reviewed 21 October 2026 against the [Flemish recognition
overview](https://www.vlaanderen.be/erkenning-van-een-school-of-andere-onderwijsinstelling),
[recognition-inspection process](https://www.vlaanderen.be/onderwijsprofessionals/organisatie-en-administratie/onderwijskwaliteit-en-toezicht/toezicht-op-de-onderwijskwaliteit/erkenningsonderzoek-scholen-academies-en-centra)
and [recognised versus unrecognised institutions](https://www.vlaanderen.be/onderwijs-en-vorming/kwaliteitszorg-in-het-onderwijs/erkende-en-niet-erkende-onderwijsinstellingen).
The [current primary-school circular BaO/97/3](https://data-onderwijs.vlaanderen.be/edulex/document.aspx?docid=9284)
links recognition conditions to the Basic Education Decree and was amended on
6 July 2026. It flags legislative caveats around the 2026–27 application
dates, so this checklist does not assert one deadline for every school.
Recognition is distinct from public funding. A Flemish-recognised school may
be privately funded; an unrecognised private school cannot issue
Flemish-recognised study certificates, and an externally recognised school
needs its own authority review. The site profile therefore records the
declared school recognition route separately from education Community, sector
and funding. Changing Community or operating jurisdiction clears this
Community-relative declaration.

Catalogue 1.40.0 adds one conditional statutory action to hold and maintain
the relevant Flemish recognition decision for an actual school in Flanders or
Brussels that declares Flemish Community recognition. An unknown route or
missing Region leaves it unresolved; unrecognised and externally recognised
routes do not receive this action. Their obligations and family-facing
information need a separate primary-source review. French and German-speaking
school recognition, provider subtypes, inspection details and municipal rules
also remain incomplete; no Belgian coverage cell is marked complete.

## French Community school network and outside-network route

Reviewed 21 October 2026 against the [current French Community education
code](https://refli.be/fr/lex/2019A30854), especially Articles 1.3.1-1,
1.5.1-2, 1.5.1-5, 1.7.1-10 and 1.7.1-12, and the [federal compulsory-age
guidance](https://www.belgium.be/fr/formation/enseignement/droits_et_devoirs/obligation_scolaire).
The code distinguishes schools organised by WBE, schools subsidised by the
French Community, and education outside that network. These categories are
not equivalent to the portal's ACE status, private/public funding shorthand,
or the Flemish recognition decision. A separate site-profile declaration
records the route. The statutory school-project and attendance-register
actions apply only to a declared organised or subsidised school in Brussels or
Wallonia. Portal attendance is an evidence signal and does not prove reporting
to the Community's system.

For a compulsory-age child educated outside an organised or subsidised school,
Article 1.7.1-12 places the annual home-education declaration on the
responsible adults, who may delegate submission to the private school's
director under their own responsibility. The catalogue therefore gives an
outside-network school serving ages five to 17 a **recommendation** to explain
the route and record the submission arrangement. It does not mislabel the
parents' duty as a universal school duty. The official annual deadline and
exceptions should be confirmed with the authority for the current school
year. Unknown age or route leaves applicability unresolved. An outside-network
learning centre that is not legally a school still requires separate review.
School admission to subsidies, special education, German-speaking provision,
and regional/municipal duties remain open; no coverage cell is closed.
