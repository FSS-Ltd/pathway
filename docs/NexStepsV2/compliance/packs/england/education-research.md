## Classification and representation

ACE participation does not establish legal school status. Public Learning Centre
means non-ACE; it does not establish state funding. CHURCH, CHARITY and CLUB can
operate education or childcare, so their sector labels cannot remove conditional
duties. NURSERY is a research sector; its stored sector remains SCHOOL with the
nursery vertical. Apply actual provider category across all six research sectors.

The current profile cannot establish compulsory school age from integer ages,
pupil numbers, EHC/looked-after status, teaching hours/content, maintained school
versus academy status, admission-authority responsibilities, section 41 approval,
which early-years register applies, precise exemptions, or acceptance of funded
early-years places. `fundingStatus=PUBLIC` is not a substitute for a specific local
authority funding agreement. These are **missing applicability facts**, not proof
that duties are absent. Publish classification-review actions with clear evidence
and preserve explicit coverage gaps for finer obligations. Do not label a broad
STATE_SCHOOL item as a universal maintained-school duty.

DfE explains that full-time is a factual assessment; 18 daytime hours per week is
an indicator, **not a safe harbour** below that number. Compulsory school age ends
on the statutory leaving date, not simply the sixteenth birthday. An institution
solely above compulsory school age cannot register as an independent school.
[DfE registration guidance, paragraphs 1.7–1.18](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6aaa9c8cf1f8d2a39605f8c9/Registration_of_independent_schools.pdf).

## Maintained-school and academy complaints distinction

[DfE maintained-school complaints guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/school-complaints-procedures/best-practice-advice-for-school-complaints-procedures-2019),
updated 2021-01-15, explains Education Act2002 section29: maintained schools and
maintained nursery schools must establish and publicise a procedure for complaints
not governed by another statutory procedure, having regard to Secretary of State
guidance. Website publication rules have specified exceptions, including maintained
nursery schools. Evidence: tailored published procedure, governing-body ownership,
complaint records and separate admissions/exclusions routes.

[DfE academy complaints guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/setting-up-an-academies-complaints-procedure/best-practice-guidance-for-academies-complaints-procedures),
updated 2021-03-12, requires academies/trusts to meet ISS Part7: informal, written
formal and independent-member panel stages. Evidence: tailored procedure and
complaint decisions. Both sources are England guidance, retrieved2026-10-15,
review due2027-01-12; exact original publication and individual legal commencement
dates are not established in this review. Do not assign the maintained-school
procedure to every STATE_SCHOOL until subtype is known.

for school registration enquiries, attendance/CME returns, SEND/local offer,
funded early-education provider agreements and admissions coordination. This review
does **not** review every council's contract, referral form or local procedure.
`localAuthority` is therefore a necessary operational fact; retain regional/local
coverage gaps rather than claiming all English local conditions reviewed.

non-ACE LEARNING_CENTRE with the same actual category receives identical legal
school duties. Test negative: PUBLIC_LEARNING_CENTRE, PRIVATE funding,
OUT_OF_SCHOOL category is never treated as STATE_SCHOOL. Test unresolved: missing
provider category, childcare status, ages, or England nation must not silently
resolve duties. `minimumAge=5, maximumAge=16, FULL_TIME` does not establish pupil
numbers or exact compulsory-age status. `minimumAge=8` does not prove all under8
registration rules irrelevant if inaccurate/incomplete ages; use confirmed profile
facts and explain boundaries. `childcare=false` does not remove school duties.
