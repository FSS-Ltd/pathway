# England source and commencement review

Reviewed 2026-10-15 for step 2.01; next review 2027-01-12. This is a bounded
source review, not legal certification or evidence of complete England coverage.
The Karpathy guidelines were applied: verify actual provisions, preserve unknowns,
and avoid speculative profile fields or universal duties.

## Retrieval and legal versions

The web reader rejected legislation.gov.uk XHTML. Read-only Python urllib retrieval
of the official `/data.xml` endpoints succeeded. The reviewer inspected the
legislative body and amendment/commencement annotations separately. Original made
PDFs are not consolidated law. XML editorial metadata dates are not commencement.
Outstanding-effect lists must be read with their notes: an entry can describe an
instrument revoked before commencement rather than a missing live amendment.

## Material correction for the catalogue

Include an explicit school force/seclusion/restraint action or disclose that scope
as unreviewed. Current ISS para 16A requires recording procedures and reasonable
steps to comply, with written seclusion/restraint records as soon as practicable.
[Para 32A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/3283/schedule/paragraph/32A/data.xml)
requires information about incidents to each parent as soon as practicable,
subject to pupil age 20+ and significant-harm exceptions. Where no parent can be
informed without likely significant harm, inform the ordinarily resident local
authority. Supply staff and volunteers are included in the para 16A staff meaning.
[Education and Inspections Act s93A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/93A/data.xml)
was also read: its use-of-force recording trigger is each **significant** incident.
Do not describe all physical contact as a reportable incident or omit the harm
exception. Follow-up inspection of the section annotation and SI 2025/1341 article 2 confirmed commencement on **1 April 2026**, through ASCL Act 2009 section 246. The catalogue records the confirmed date.

## Provider terms and practical applicability

The primary provider pages [ICCE registration](https://www.icce.education/school-hea-registration)
and [moderation](https://www.icce.education/moderation-1) were independently
retrieved. They concern participating registered entities and quality assurance;
they are provider requirements, not English legislation or obligations on all ACE
users. The safeguarding research's enrolment, training and moderation detail
still needs the cited page/PDF and actual regional agreement for each final item.
Do not infer ICCE participation from ACE, or invent a universal ACE renewal cycle.
ACE-only terms remain an explicit gap pending actual provider agreement review.
Provider publication/retrieval dates do not establish contract effective dates.

Minimal profile facts such as actual school category, employment, premises control,
confirmed food-business status, own vehicle operation and EYFS applicability help
select review actions. They do not settle specialist exceptions. Driver entitlement,
food-operation frequency, fire responsibility allocation, registration exemptions,
regulated activity and local permission decisions belong in assessed evidence;
missing facts must produce unresolved applicability rather than invented duties.

The education, safeguarding and operations research correctly distinguish guidance
from law and retain local/specialist gaps. This review does not independently
validate every source in those files: attendance/EYFS/RSHE current editions, food,
transport, equality, boarding, childcare exemptions and all local conditions remain
subject to their recorded source review and boundaries. Planning, building control,
local safeguarding arrangements and specialist activity licences are not certified
by this national core. A published bounded core can coexist with IN_REVIEW coverage;
full England coverage and the complete step must not be asserted from this review.

## Catalogue date semantics

`effective.from` remains the known legal/application start. A nullable, optional
`effective.currentAsOf` records independently verified current applicability of
text with unknown commencement; it never substitutes a retrieval date for legal
commencement. Unknown commencement still requires its explanation. Observations
cannot postdate retrieval or verification and expire with the source review.
A known future start always prevents current selection, even with an observation.
A reviewed state or successful URL retrieval alone never establishes applicability.

## School medical and allergy update

Current [CFA2014 section100 XML](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/6/section/100/data.xml)
was read: duty commenced 2014-09-01 (SI2014/889 article7(c)). Actual statutory
school categories include maintained schools, Academy schools, alternative
provision Academies and PRUs; maintained nursery schools and16-to-19 academies
are outside that definition and young children are excluded by subsection3.
Neither integer ages nor selected vertical establishes these legal facts.

Current [section100A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/6/section/100A/data.xml),
section100B and [SI2026/933 regulation2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/933/regulation/2/data.xml)
were read. Substantive allergy-policy duties commenced **2026-09-01**. Annual
review, annual publicity to pupils/parents/all workers and website publication
are already statutory. The July2026 allergy guidance has future-tense passages
about annual review/publicity: current section100A takes precedence. Its separate
training/spare-device proposals are not converted into universal current duties.

The actual current December2015 medical-guidance PDF and July2026 allergy PDF
were read for scope, plans, medicines, competence, policy, training and incidents.
Text-box medical advice remains non-statutory. Medical consultation drafts are
not substituted for current guidance. Independent equivalents of the new allergy
policy duties remain forthcoming in the reviewed DfE text; current ISS paragraph13
separately requires timely competent pupil first aid through an implemented written
policy. Employer first-aid provision does not replace it.

The medical catalogue uses an explicit assessed section100 applicability fact,
`englandSchoolMedicalDutiesApply`, to avoid deriving statutory scope from screens,
funding, ages or broad school category. Missing assessment remains unresolved.
