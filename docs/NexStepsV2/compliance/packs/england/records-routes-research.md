## Separate access routes

**General personal-data access:** pupil, employee, volunteer, adult learner and service-user rights arise under UK GDPR Article15, not from account type or parental status. Use the already-reviewed current Article12A deadline rules (DUAA section76; effective5February2026), rather than freezing the older ICO education page's shorthand into the application. School holidays do not suspend SAR deadlines. Parent access on a child's behalf requires authority/competence assessment; parental portal access is not blanket authority. GDPR access and the education-specific route can apply to the same request. Evidence: identity/authority assessment, request classification, separate deadline calculation, searches, justified redactions/exemptions and secure response record.

[ICO education-information guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/right-of-access/education-information/) was last updated8December2025. Its broad maintained-school summary is incomplete for the precise England regulation below; use the legislation for institution scope. Classification: regulator guidance, currentAsOf2026-10-15, original effective date not asserted.

**Education-specific parental access:** [Education (Pupil Information) (England) Regulations2005, regulation5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1437/regulation/5/data.xml) applies to governing bodies of LA-maintained schools **excluding nursery schools**, and **special schools not so maintained**. Free inspection within15 school days of written parental request; copies within15 school days, with a fee no greater than supply cost if charged. Regulation5(4) excludes material the pupil could not lawfully obtain under GDPR. These are statutory duties, not a universal15-day rule for every school or every adult record. Ordinary academies/free schools and ordinary independent schools do not acquire this route merely from being schools; independently check the special-school scope rather than excluding all non-maintained provision. Actual legal classification and record/request type govern, not ACE or product sector. Evidence: exact classification and requester/record scope, dates, lawful exclusions, fee calculation and response.

**Keep and transfer education records:** [regulation4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1437/regulation/4/data.xml) requires covered maintained schools excluding nurseries and non-maintained special schools to keep curricular records updated at least once each school year. This is **not** a one-year retention deadline. [Regulation9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1437/regulation/9/data.xml) has a distinct maintained-school transfer regime: generally15 school days after leaving for another registered school, plus dual-registration/request rules and specified exceptions. Do not substitute a generic15-calendar-day-after-confirmation rule or impose this precise statutory route on all academies without their own applicable terms. Regulation5(5) separately addresses curricular-record requests when admission elsewhere is being considered. Retain receipt/secure transfer evidence and handle safeguarding files separately under the relevant safeguarding guidance.

2005 Regulations commenced8July2005 under [regulation1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1437/regulation/1/data.xml); current amended text checked, but individual amendment commencement dates not all re-established. Use currentAsOf plus that limitation where source metadata otherwise implies an unchanged2005 version.

**Education exemptions are not total exclusions:** [DPA2018 Schedule3 Part4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12/schedule/3/part/4/data.xml), paragraphs14/17/19, covers a broader educational-record definition including academies, independent and non-maintained special schools. Education data excludes health data for this special definition. The serious-harm restriction is to the extent disclosure would likely cause serious physical/mental health harm; it is not a licence to withhold all safeguarding records. Teacher-only personal-use notes can fall outside this educational-record definition without automatically being exempt from general GDPR rights. Statutory, currentAsOf2026-10-15; all amendment dates not separately established. Evidence: record-by-record exemption reasoning, not a categorical exclusion.

## Retention: distinguish law, guidance and business justification

[School Attendance (Pupil Registration) (England) Regulations2024 regulation7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/208/regulation/7/data.xml) expressly requires admission/attendance register entries preserved for **six years from entry**, and required backups for **six years after the school year to which they relate**. These are distinct clocks, not six years from leaving. Apply within that instrument's school scope; a learning centre that is not legally a school is not automatically captured. Exact commencement **19August2024** verified in [regulation1 XML](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/208/regulation/1/data.xml).

[DfE school record-management guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/data-protection-in-schools/record-keeping-and-management) sits in a manual updated14October2026. It recommends pupil-file/child-protection retention benchmarks and provides a mixed schedule labelled statutory. **Do not import that label wholesale.** Its suggested25th-birthday pupil-file benchmark is not a period stated in the2005 Regulations. [Limitation Act1980 section2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1980/58/section/2/data.xml) is a six-year tort limitation provision, not an express requirement to retain every pupil/staff record for that period. Limitation, safeguarding, claims and operational need may inform documented retention decisions, with actual statutory duties and holds taking precedence. The DfE manual update is not evidence of new legislation effective14October2026. Classify its operational benchmarks as guidance unless the exact cited law imposes that duration.

Do not mechanically apply age25 to every duplicate, catering record, mailing list or staff record. Keep category/purpose-specific periods and start events, responsible owner, transfer/disposal rules, legal/preservation holds and periodic review. Pseudonymised data may remain personal data; removing a name is not proof of anonymisation. Do not implement automatic deletion on the basis of this research alone.

For child-sexual-abuse material, do not convert the IICSA recommendation into a universal new75-year statutory requirement. [Government progress response](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/tackling-child-sexual-abuse-progress-update/tackling-child-sexual-abuse-progress-update-accessible-version) expressly distinguishes a proposed code/best practice from mandating75 years. [Parliament answer2June2026](https://questions-statements.parliament.uk/written-questions/detail/2026-05-18/2025) still describes regulations/code as forthcoming. This search did not establish a later final code's commencement; preserve that uncertainty and any actual hold/sector duty. Birthday-based recommendations and75 years from a record/event are not interchangeable.

## Adult and service-sector records

General GDPR purpose/necessity, access and retention controls apply across churches, charities, clubs and learning centres processing adult personal data. School parental-access provisions do not create a spouse/next-of-kin right to adult records. Identify lawful representative authority rather than treating family relationship as sufficient. Sector alone cannot determine a universal6/8/25/75-year schedule.

When actual provision is a regulated health/social-care activity, [CQC regulation17 guidance](https://www.cqc.org.uk/guidance-regulation/providers/regulations-service-providers-and-managers/health-social-care-act/regulation-17) identifies statutory17(2)(c)/(d) duties for secure, accurate, complete, contemporaneous care records and relevant staff/management records. The [current regulation17 XML](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/2936/regulation/17/data.xml) was also directly verified; it does not impose a universal eight-year period on every charity supporting adults. CurrentAsOf2026-10-15; original/amendment commencement dates not all established. Scope depends on actual regulated activity/provider obligations. Evidence: regulator/service classification, applicable record categories and current commissioned/professional schedule, representative authority, holds and disposal controls.

The NHS England Records Management Code official URL failed retrieval in this pass. Do not publish specific NHS/social-care eight-year minima from third-party copies. Detailed regulated-care retention, health/social-work access exemptions, deceased-person access, adoption/looked-after records and contractual commissioning requirements remain separate bounded research gaps. No new care-sector product is proposed.

Suggested evidence: institution/service classification; category-to-source schedule with precise start event and duty/guidance distinction; documented request routing; required transfer receipts; holds, secure disposal and unresolved scope/remediation. Do not permit confirmation while a required route or action remains unresolved. Existing profile facts cannot reliably distinguish every non-maintained special-school/care subcategory; evidence-led assessment avoids inventing a false universal predicate. No new flags requested. England coverage remains partial; this does not certify every record type or service regime.
