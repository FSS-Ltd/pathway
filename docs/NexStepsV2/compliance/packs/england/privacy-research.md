## Applicability

UK GDPR/DPA duties apply UK-wide; this pack selects **England / GB-ENG**.
SCHOOL, NURSERY, CHURCH, CHARITY, CLUB and LEARNING_CENTRE can all be controllers.
NURSERY is a research category; do not invent a runtime sector. Schools/childcare
operated by churches, charities or clubs retain conditional institution duties.
ACE participation and public funding do not establish legal school/public-authority
status. Public Learning Centre means non-ACE, not necessarily publicly funded.

Current profile lacks controller/processor/joint-controller role, actual public-body
status, purposes/lawful bases, processing scale/frequency/data types, direct online
services to children, automated decisions, fee exemption facts, transfers and actual
school category. These are missing applicability facts, not proof duties are absent.
Use classification-review actions where unsupported; do not assert DPO/DPIA/fee
obligations solely from sector labels.

## Gaps and promotion boundaries

Research verification relied on primary-source search extracts rather than full
snapshots. Browser XHTML retrieval failed, but official XML retrieval subsequently succeeded.
Before publication verify exact amended Articles 6/10/12A, DPA Schedule 1 and
biometric exceptions. Articles 9/12/30 and DPA section 164A were read in XML.
Official S.I. 2026/82 regulation 3(a) XML confirms 19 June 2026 commencement; this is not a substitute
for a section-by-section DUAA commencement audit. Most remaining data-protection
provisions commenced 5 February 2026 per ICO, with complaints still future then.
Do not claim every DUAA effect commenced on assent or in February.

Further scoped review: DUAA recognised legitimate interests, children's-service
design changes, significant automated decisions, PECR cookies/electronic marketing
and charity soft opt-in, current transfers test, children's-code exclusions,
record-specific education/EYFS/safeguarding durations and ecclesiastical records.
These are explicit gaps, not findings that no duties apply. Catalogue checks cannot
prove security, deletion, rights handling or live incident deadline compliance.

Documentation-only research changes no runtime, persistence, progress calculation
or tenant permissions. Those checks remain with step owner. No runtime tests,
build, commit or PR performed; parent owns consolidation and delivery evidence.
