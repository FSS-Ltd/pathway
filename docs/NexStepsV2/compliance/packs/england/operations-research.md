Research revision: `england-operations-2026-10-15-r1`. Researcher: Codex
England operations agent. Retrieved/verified: **2026-10-15**. Next source review:

## Applicability decision

Investigated sectors: SCHOOL, NURSERY, LEARNING_CENTRE, CHURCH, CHARITY and CLUB.
The same activity can create duties in any sector. ACE, charitable status and
Public Learning Centre identity do not establish employer status, government
funding, food-business status or transport exemptions.

The current profile cannot express employees/headcount, control of premises,
food-business status, specific school category, vehicle/operator facts or
residential-special-school status. Publish broad classification/safety review
recommendations where justified; do not describe conditional statutory duties as
unconditionally applicable. Keep the additional duties and local approvals as
explicit coverage gaps until the necessary facts and source review are recorded.

## Regional and activity boundaries

National duties above do not establish that a particular site has permission to
operate. Council environmental health decides borderline food-business activity;
fire and rescue authorities enforce general fire precautions. Planning/change of
use, building control, licensing of particular activities and conditions on local
permissions require site-specific checks. There is no reviewed inventory of all
English council policies in this note. Keep regional coverage unresolved with a
request for the relevant authority/permission record rather than marking England
fully covered by these national sources.

Current minibus guidance distinguishes a 3,500kg MAM limit from 4,250kg for
electric/hydrogen vehicles and an additional disabled-passenger-equipment
allowance. Older school advice presents the earlier simplified limit. Link the
current DVLA page and record actual entitlement/vehicle facts; do not calculate
licence eligibility from these profile flags. Seat-belt fitment and wearing rules
also differ by vehicle first-use date, passenger age, seating and vehicle class.

Unreviewed specialist subtopics: asbestos, legionella, electrical/gas plant,
construction, hazardous substances, adventure-activity licensing exceptions,
passenger-service international operation, residential special schools,
children's-home registration and private-fostering arrangements. They remain
explicit coverage gaps; general hazard review does not certify these duties.

## Applicability fixtures for publication review

- England ACE independent school: actual independent-school premises duties apply;
  ACE status creates no exemption. Section 100 medical guidance is not inferred.
- England non-ACE learning centre with volunteers only: employer first-aid/risk
  duties unresolved until actual employment/undertaking facts; common-law care and
  controlled non-domestic premises still assessed. Not government-funded by name.
- Charity with one annual cake event: food classification review applies; do not
  automatically demand registration, allergen labelling or hygiene certificates.
- Charity supplying meals weekly: review confirms food-business classification and
  council registration/safety obligations, even if meals are free.
- School ordinary daytime curriculum visit for ten-year-olds: no blanket separate
  written-consent action; nursery-age/higher-risk/out-of-hours test differs.
- Transport contracted from licensed operator: check the provider; do not demand
  the site obtains its own section 19 permit merely because `transport=true`.
- Boarding flag with unknown legal provider category: ordinary boarding-school
  standards unresolved; do not assume school or residential-special-school regime.
- Wales/Scotland/NI or missing nation: no England requirement selected by fallback.

explanation, but consolidated amendment/commencement text was not independently
