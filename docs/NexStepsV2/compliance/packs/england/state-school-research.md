# England maintained/academy national-core closure research

Verified 2026-10-15; next source review 2027-01-12. Prepared for current step 2.01. No runtime, repository or database changes made. Primary official legislation XML was inspected alongside current DfE guidance. This is a bounded national-core proposal; it is not an assertion that every school-specific agreement, exemption or operational policy has been examined.

## Encoding and classification

Use the existing actual `schoolCategory` facts: `MAINTAINED` and `ACADEMY`. Do not substitute product vertical, SCHOOL sector, public funding, or the broad `providerCategory=STATE_SCHOOL` for the finer classification. Unknown schoolCategory must leave the respective action unresolved. All six research sectors may operate the relevant legal school.

No new flags are needed for the actions below. Where subtypes differ, phrase the action as a documented scope assessment **and performance of duties found applicable**, with the precise branches stated. A legally required universal action may be STATUTORY_DUTY; a generic scope-assessment instruction is a RECOMMENDATION even when it describes conditional statutory duties. Do not relabel an entire conditional bundle as an unconditional statutory duty. External academy funding terms are contractual requirements, not automatically legislation; PROVIDER_REQUIREMENT can describe funder requirements if the UI's label allows that meaning, otherwise use an assessment RECOMMENDATION and explain the binding funding agreement in the detail.

A completed assessment must include its conclusion, applicable provisions, implementation evidence and any exemption rationale. It cannot mean merely that someone looked at a source. The national rules below can be reviewed even while institution-specific evidence is unresolved.

## 2026 commencement findings

1. [Children's Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026 s54](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/21/section/54/data.xml) would amend Academies Act s1A to require the National Curriculum. Its XML annotation says in force at Royal Assent **for specified purposes**, referring to [s78(1)(a), (5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/21/section/78/data.xml). Those are regulation-making powers and appointed commencement, not a current teaching obligation. [Current Academies Act 2010 s1A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/1A/data.xml) still has the broad/balanced curriculum wording without the new National Curriculum clause. Do **not** publish a present blanket National Curriculum duty for academies. The government's policy timetable refers to September 2028, but a policy plan is not a verified legal commencement date. Keep the future change in source-review monitoring.
2. [CWS Act s61](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/21/section/61/data.xml) **is in force 2026-06-29**, per its annotation and s78(2). It inserts SSFA1998 s85ZA (admissions cooperation) and Education Act1996 s19B (foreseeable decisions affecting adequate education provision). Include this current operational duty; do not miss it by using a pre-2026 governance checklist.
3. [s62](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/21/section/62/data.xml) and [s63](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/21/section/63/data.xml) retain “not in force at Royal Assent”/appointed commencement notes; [s64](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/21/section/64/data.xml) is only specified-purpose commencement. This review did not establish substantive commencement for these provisions. Do not present proposed admission-direction/adjudicator reforms as currently active solely because the Act was enacted. Recheck current commencement instruments before any future publication of these additional reforms.
4. The **Academy Trust Handbook 2026 applies from 2026-10-01**, not September: [official current handbook](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/academy-trust-handbook/academy-trust-handbook-2026-effective-from-1-october-2026). The source expressly makes compliance a funding-agreement condition. Detailed finance/accounting remains outside this version; operational governance is in scope.

## Draft checklist actions

### A. Maintained operational governance

**Condition:** schoolCategory MAINTAINED. **Class:** STATUTORY_DUTY for the regulation-6 baseline; separate recommendations from current governance guidance.

**Action:** “Maintain the governing body's strategic oversight, a competent clerk, clear headteacher accountability, recorded decisions and effective delegated responsibilities.”

**Authority/provisions:** Secretary of State for Education, [School Governance (Roles, Procedures and Allowances) (England) Regulations 2013 reg6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/1624/regulation/6/data.xml). Core functions include strategy and educational-performance oversight; integrity, objectivity and openness; appointing a clerk and having regard to the clerk's advice. Reg6 annotation: effective **2013-09-01**. [DfE maintained governance guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/maintained-schools-governance-guide/maintained-schools-governance-guide), updated **2026-07-15**, covers maintained special/nursery schools as well as community/foundation/voluntary schools; it does not directly apply to PRUs. The guide is guidance combining duties and recommendations, not a new commencement instrument.

**Evidence:** current instrument of government, governor/committee responsibilities, clerk appointment, decision/delegation records, educational-performance review. Financial governance detail excluded from this pack's claim. Federation and temporary-body arrangements are institution-specific evidence questions, not reasons to mark the national baseline wholly unreviewed.

### B. Academy operational governance and applicable funding terms

**Condition:** schoolCategory ACADEMY. **Class:** contractual/funder requirement for actual handbook obligations; RECOMMENDATION for the umbrella agreement assessment.

**Action:** “Identify the trust's current articles, funding agreement and delegated responsibilities; demonstrate operational governance, trustee oversight and compliance with applicable current handbook requirements.”

**Sources:** [Academy Trust Handbook 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/academy-trust-handbook/academy-trust-handbook-2026-effective-from-1-october-2026), introduction/Part1, current from **2026-10-01**; [academy governance guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/academy-trust-governance-guide/academy-trust-governance-guide), updated **2026-07-20**, governance and leadership. Authority DfE. The handbook applies across trust types and distinguishes must from should. The announced future trust standards are not yet a substitute for the current framework. Original publication dates not independently established for each edition; record unknown rather than using updated date as initial publication.

**Evidence:** articles/funding agreement and versions, trustee/member register, scheme of delegation, governance professional appointment, board oversight records and required operational publications. Trust-level evidence may support multiple sites without changing the site attestation boundary. Actual contractual variations need operator evidence. Corporate financial/tax/payroll work remains excluded.

### C. Maintained complaints, including maintained nurseries

**Condition:** schoolCategory MAINTAINED. **Class:** STATUTORY_DUTY.

**Action:** “Establish and publicise complaints procedures for the school and its community facilities/services, directing complaints with separate statutory procedures to the correct route.”

**Source:** Parliament, [Education Act2002 s29(1)–(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29/data.xml), effective **2003-09-01** in England, per SI2003/1667art4 annotation. [s39](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/39/data.xml) definition includes maintained nursery schools. Crucial: the nursery exclusion in **s29(4) applies only to s29(3)** (off-site curriculum), not complaints. Governing bodies have regard to relevant Secretary of State guidance. Online publication under School Information rules has its own narrower scope (see H); “publicise” is the universal s29 wording.

**Evidence:** adopted procedure, accessible communication route, staff allocation, records of correct signposting for admissions/exclusions/SEND matters and other alternative statutory procedures. Confidential complaint records remain restricted. The current maintained governance guide §7.13 supports this interpretation.

### D. Academy complaints: distinguish 16–19 institutions

**Condition:** schoolCategory ACADEMY. **Class:** RECOMMENDATION for subtype assessment + implement applicable statutory/funding duties; do not make ISSPart7 universal to every possible ACADEMY subtype.

**Action:** “Confirm the academy subtype and implement the applicable complaints route; where ISS Part7 applies, provide informal, written and independent-panel stages with prescribed records and availability.”

**Sources:** [ISS Schedule para33](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/3283/schedule/paragraph/33/data.xml), current regulation standard; original regulations effective2015-01-05 (later amendments do not share that date automatically). DfE [academy complaints guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/setting-up-an-academies-complaints-procedure), first published2014-03-11, updated2021-03-12; page explicitly says continue guidance after ESFA closure. Current academy governance guide §7.3 and [academy publication guidance, Complaints](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/what-academies-free-schools-and-colleges-should-publish-online) explicitly exempt **16–19 academies** from this ISS complaints requirement and recommend an accessible complaints procedure for them. Available-to-parents/on-request duty is not identical to a universal statutory website-publication duty.

**Evidence:** subtype/funding basis, procedure/timeframes, panel independence arrangements, confidential register and availability record. Do not infer 16–19 legal status from min/max age alone.

### E. Maintained curriculum, phase and exemptions

**Condition:** schoolCategory MAINTAINED. **Class:** STATUTORY_DUTY for broad/balanced baseline, with explicit assessment and implementation of conditional curriculum components.

**Action:** “Provide a balanced, broadly based curriculum; document the school's phase and apply the National Curriculum, religious education and relationships/health requirements that legally apply, retaining any lawful exception.”

**Sources:** Parliament, [EA2002 s78](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/78/data.xml), broad/balanced development/preparation baseline expressly covers maintained schools **and maintained nursery schools**, wholly commenced2002-10-01. [s80](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/80/data.xml) defines basic curriculum and pupil-age/phase exceptions; national curriculum applies to registered pupils who have ceased to be young children under Childcare ActPart1 and are not over compulsory school age. RE and relationships/health have their own exceptions, including under-compulsory-age and special-school RE provisions. Current RSHE amendments came into force2020-09-01, not every provision on original Act date. [Maintained guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/maintained-schools-governance-guide/maintained-schools-governance-guide) curriculum section cross-checks current arrangements.

**Evidence:** phase/legal-scope assessment; curriculum map and subject plans; EYFS route where applicable; withdrawal/disapplication decisions; SEN adaptations and implementation review. Nursery-only provision must not be told to teach every national-curriculum subject because it selects MAINTAINED. Ages in portal support assessment but do not determine legal phase by themselves.

### F. Academy curriculum: current rather than prospective law

**Condition:** schoolCategory ACADEMY. **Class:** RECOMMENDATION for subtype/agreement assessment, or split known applicable current statutory/contractual action.

**Action:** “Identify the academy's legal subtype and funding requirements and implement its applicable broad and balanced curriculum, subject and assessment duties; retain lawful adaptations.”

**Sources:** [Academies Act2010 s1A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/1A/data.xml), current text verified; relevant insertions wholly commenced2012-04-01. Mainstream academy-school route s1A(1)(b) incorporates EA2002s78; s1A(2) separately addresses special academies, and s1A excludes alternative-provision academies (s1C). Exact agreement evidence matters for individual curriculum obligations. Use current academy governance guide curriculum section as guidance. **Do not apply the uncommenced substantive CWS2026s54 National Curriculum amendment.**

**Evidence:** subtype decision, agreement curriculum clauses, implemented curriculum/assessment map and exceptions. Remaining trust-agreement particulars are site evidence, not an unreviewed national duty. Separate 16–19/AP/early-years academy detail cannot be fabricated from broad category.

### G. SEND support, SENCO and EHCP admission: distinct scopes

**Condition:** maintained or academy category for assessment; independent institutions need a separate assessment for section41 approval/named EHCP, not an inferred duty on all independent schools. **Class:** RECOMMENDATION for applicability assessment; STATUTORY_DUTY only for an accurately scoped sub-action.

**Action:** “Document which SEND duties apply to this institution; implement required SEN support, SENCO arrangements and information reporting, and admit a child or young person when an applicable named-EHCP duty requires it.”

Primary sources (Parliament; original listed substantive duties commenced **2014-09-01**, SI2014/889art7(a), later amendments separately dated):

- [CFA2014s66](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/6/section/66/data.xml): best endeavours where a registered pupil/student has SEN; scope mainstream schools, maintained nurseries,16–19 academies,AP academies,FE and PRUs. [s83](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/6/section/83/data.xml) defines mainstream to exclude special schools; “maintained school” definition also excludes hospital-established special schools. Do not state s66 applies to every special school merely because it is maintained.
- [s67](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/6/section/67/data.xml): designate SENCO in mainstream schools and maintained nurseries. DfE [SENCO qualification guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/special-educational-needs-co-ordinator-senco-national-professional-qualification) and [transition guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/mandatory-qualification-for-sencos/transition-to-national-professional-qualification-for-special-educational-needs-co-ordinators) explain NPQ transition from2024-09-01, three-year completion window and existing qualification/experience exemptions. Do not demand a fresh NPQ from every established SENCO. Exact staff appointment dates/qualifications are evidence.
- [s69](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/6/section/69/data.xml): SEN information report for maintained schools/nurseries and Academy schools; includes disabled admissions/accessibility information. [SEND Regulations2014reg51](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/1530/regulation/51/data.xml)/Schedule1 give report content and hospital-special-school exclusion. [Regulation52](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/1530/regulation/52/made/data.xml), subsequently verified both directly and within the current whole-instrument XML, requires publication on the school website. Both regs51/52 carry current XML start2014-09-01. Reg52 itself does not impose an annual update interval: distinguish the current guidance/review expectation from the publication duty.
- [s43](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/6/section/43/data.xml): admit where named in an EHC plan for listed institutions, including maintained schools/nurseries,academies,non-maintained special schools and **section41-approved** independent institutions. A private school's product label does not establish section41 approval.
- [s77](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/6/section/77/data.xml): listed bodies have regard to SEND Code when exercising Part3 functions. Current [SEND Code publication](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/send-code-of-practice-0-to-25) is statutory guidance. Approval/body definitions matter; not blanket every private tuition provider.

**Evidence:** institution-type/approval assessment, SEN policy and graduated support review, SENCO appointment/qualification exemption or deadline, SEN information report/publication, EHCP naming/admission decisions (restricted). Individual EHCP needs and exact appointment history are inherently institution evidence. The publication duty is directly verified; any asserted update interval needs its separate source.

### H. Operational publication: distinguish duties, recommendations and nursery scope

**Conditions:** MAINTAINED and ACADEMY in separate assessment actions. **Class:** recommendation to assess applicable publication schedule; identify each binding statutory or contractual component explicitly.

**Action:** “Maintain an applicable-information publication register; publish and update required operational information, and record the basis for items outside this school's phase or legal category.”

**Maintained sources:** [School Information Regulations2008reg2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/3093/regulation/2/data.xml) definition forParts3/4 covers community/foundation/voluntary and non-hospital community/foundation special schools, **not maintained nursery schools by that definition**. Original2008reg10 XML is **unamended text**, so it must not be treated as the current prospectus duty. [2012/1124reg2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/1124/regulation/2/data.xml) substitutes website publication, free paper copies on request and reasonably-practicable/at-least-annual updating, with admissions-specific timing; introduces Schedule 4. Regulation 1 was additionally verified: the 2012 amendments commenced 2012-09-01. [2016/451reg2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/451/regulation/2/data.xml) adds complaints and changes performance/PupilPremium detail, effective2016-09-01. [2025/600reg2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/600/regulation/2/data.xml) updates the financial benchmarking link (financial detail outside scope). [DfE maintained publication guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/what-maintained-schools-must-publish-online), current page last updated2024-10-24, is an operational overview, not a substitute for amended law. Its wording that paper copies “should” be provided differs from the explicit2012 regulation “must”; prefer the statute within its scope.

**Academy sources:** [DfE academy publication guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/what-academies-free-schools-and-colleges-should-publish-online), published2016-06-30/updated2024-10-24, distinguishes funding requirements, additional legal requirements and recommended publication. Do not turn all its “should” headings into statute. Handbook2026 and actual funding agreement add current governance requirements.

**Evidence:** public URL/date checks for contacts, applicable curriculum, admissions, complaints availability, governance information, inspection reports, SEND/accessibility and applicable assessment outputs; reasons for phase/category exclusions. Keep detailed financial publications outside this version. Precise performance metrics change by cohort and publication year: link the current applicable schedule rather than permanently hardcoding old2012metrics. A maintained nursery may have separate complaints/SEND duties despite being outside the2008Part4definition; do not show “no obligations”.

### I. Admissions authority and applicable code

**Condition:** MAINTAINED or ACADEMY, with explicit subtype/authority assessment. **Class:** RECOMMENDATION assessment; applicable Code requirements STATUTORY_GUIDANCE, underlying legal duties distinguished.

**Action:** “Identify the admission authority and applicable admissions route; implement its consultation, determination, publication, application and appeal duties, or cooperate with the responsible authority where the school is not that authority.”

**Sources:** [SSFA1998s88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/31/section/88/data.xml): community/voluntary-controlled normallyLA (unless agreed delegation); foundation/voluntary-aided governing body; academy-school proprietor. Governing body must implementLAadmission decisions under s88(1A) (inserted2007-01-08 in England). [s88C](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/31/section/88C/data.xml): determine/consult/notify; whole applicable insertion from2009-01-26. [DfE School Admissions Code](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/school-admissions-code--2), current2021code effective2021-09-01, is statutory guidance; the current landing page still identifies that edition. Academy publication guidance expressly excludes special academies,APandstandalone16–19institutions from the admissions/appeals codes. Nursery admission is not automatically the primary-school statutory admission route; a nursery-to-school transfer needs a separate application. Broad schoolCategory alone must not set an authority or apply every Code deadline.

**Evidence:** legal type and authority record; applicable admissions arrangements and annual calendar, consultation/determination/publication proof,LAcoordination/fair-access arrangements, application decisions and appeal route. Exact faith/selection arrangements and local admission criteria are institutional evidence. NewCWSadmission-direction reforms whose substantive commencement is not verified are excluded, not silently activated.

### J. Current2026 cooperation duties

**Condition:** MAINTAINED or ACADEMY for a scope-and-implementation action. **Class:** RECOMMENDATION assessment with explicit conditional statutory duty; actual relevant-school category must be established within evidence.

**Action:** “Identify and discharge current duties to cooperate with the local authority on admissions and decisions foreseeably affecting sufficient education provision.”

**Source:** CWS2026s61 (above), effective2026-06-29. SSFA85ZA covers respectivePartIIIfunctions of maintained-school governing bodies and Academy-school proprietors withLA. EA1996s19B applies when decisions foreseeably affectLAduties under s14 or s19(1); the relevant-school table includes community/foundation/voluntary/community-special/foundation-special,Academy school,APacademy,PRUmanagement committee. It does not list a maintained nursery or16–19academy as such. Avoid claiming all broad-category schools owe every branch.

**Evidence:** subtype/scope conclusion,LA liaison responsibilities, decision impact assessments and correspondence for relevant proposals. Actual foreseeable impacts are institution evidence; the national duty is verified and should not remain an unreviewed2026gap.

## Publication recommendation and residual boundaries

The bounded first version can cover A–J as carefully labelled assessment-and-fulfilment actions without extra flags. Direct mandatory baselines A/C/E are particularly strong; broad academy/SEND/admissions/publication bundles need the explicit branch wording above. Do not mark national coverage REVIEWED_NO_REQUIREMENTS for any excluded subtype: often a separate obligation remains.

Remaining national-detail checks before claiming exhaustive closure: complete the actual2026commencement-instrument sweep for s62–64; full current governance constitution/quorum/election rules; detailed maintained nursery/hospital special-publication routes; precise statutory curriculum disapplication and exam-phase requirements; subtype-specific academy agreements; current admissions appealsCode text. The report supplies a substantial reviewed national baseline, not a substitute for all those granular branches. Actual trust articles, school phases, special-school status, section41approval, EHCPs, namedLAs and governor allocations are institution evidence, not inherently unreviewable national sources.
