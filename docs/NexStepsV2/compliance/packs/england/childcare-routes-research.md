# England childcare registration and funded-place routes

Verified 15 October 2026; review due 12 January 2027. These are conditional
assessment recommendations across all six institutional research sectors. They
add no inference from a nursery screen, public funding label or ACE participation.

## Childcare Register conditions

Ofsted publishes separate continuing requirements for [compulsory daycare](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/daycare-providers-on-the-compulsory-childcare-register-ofsted-requirements)
and [voluntary daycare](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/daycare-providers-on-the-voluntary-childcare-register-ofsted-requirements).
Both first published 9 August 2022; current update 1 September 2026. Their legal
bases include the Childcare Act 2006 and amended General Childcare Register
Regulations. The precise current regulatory commencement history was not reviewed;
source `currentAsOf` records observation rather than inventing a single start date.

The action requires identifying the actual certificate, provider type and register
parts, then mapping and fulfilling applicable requirements. Scope includes welfare,
safeguarding, checks/training, first aid, premises, insurance, complaints, records,
parent information and notifications. Voluntary registration imposes continuing
conditions while held; EYFS and compulsory registration remain separate regimes.
No universal register ratio, retention period or role-check rule is copied onto
all childcare institutions. Evidence includes the certificate or supported exemption,
conditions matrix and relevant restricted records. The September update also
addresses banned dogs; detailed requirement mapping remains necessary.

## Funded early-years provision

[DfE's statutory guidance valid from 1 April 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/early-education-and-childcare--2/early-education-and-childcare-valid-from-1-april-2026)
is directed at local authorities. Provider responsibilities depend on the actual
local arrangement. The row assesses participation and, where relevant, implementation
of eligibility, free access, optional extras/alternatives, charging transparency,
quality, SEND and safeguarding conditions. Private providers can participate;
funding status does not establish participation. Maintained-school arrangements
have distinct provisions.

Expected evidence is the participation conclusion, current applicable local terms,
parent information and operational records. The action is a recommendation to
assess and implement the correct route; it does not relabel all local-authority
guidance as a direct statutory duty on each provider. Detailed corporate finance
remains outside scope. Site-specific agreements and register conditions remain
explicit coverage gaps rather than an assertion of no obligations.
