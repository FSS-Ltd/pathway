## Metadata and publication rules

All sources below were retrieved/read on 2026-10-15 from the issuing authority. Territory selected for this pack is GB-ENG; some HSE law is GB-wide, but this does not publish another jurisdiction. Language English, no translation. Unless a date is explicitly verified below, use publishedAt=null with reason “Original publication day not independently established”; effective.from=null, currentAsOf=2026-10-15, to=null, unknownReason explaining that consolidated/current guidance was checked but all amendment commencements were not. Do not convert retrieval, made, Royal Assent or page-update dates into legal commencement. Suggested next review 2027-01-12, sooner on change.

Recommended checklist classification matters: where an existing flag only prompts assessment, use RECOMMENDATION for the assess-and-fulfil action and explain the conditional statutory duties. Do not relabel the whole action STATUTORY_DUTY merely because some assessed cases have one. No extra questionnaire is needed for every substance, appliance or transport detail: attach a dated, accountable assessment and evidence to the item. ## Proposed premises actions

### P1 — Asbestos responsibility, assessment and management

Condition: controlsPremises=true OR employsStaff=true. RECOMMENDATION at this profile granularity.
Action: “Determine asbestos dutyholders and maintenance responsibilities; obtain a suitable premises assessment and implement the required management, information and review arrangements.”
Sources: HSE [duty to manage](https://www.hse.gov.uk/asbestos/duty/index.htm), [check responsibility](https://www.hse.gov.uk/asbestos/duty/check.htm) (second URL is a navigation reference, not independently read); [CAR 2012 regulation 4, official XML](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/632/regulation/4/data.xml), read directly.
Actual law: contractual/tenancy maintenance or repair obligations determine dutyholder extent; absent such agreements, control matters. Others must cooperate. Assessment/reviews must be recorded; if asbestos is present or presumed, assess risk and maintain/implement a written plan, inform potential disturbers and emergency services. Review after significant change/invalidity and at regular intervals. HSE recommends annual plan review; do not call 12 months a universal reg4 statutory interval. Do not require removal merely because asbestos exists, or a paid survey where reliable evidence supports a competent assessment.
Evidence: lease/control allocation, building/asbestos assessment or supported absence conclusion, register/plan where applicable, contractor information and review/repair records. Non-domestic premises and shared residential parts matter; school/charity status does not exempt them.

### P2 — Water systems and legionella

Condition: controlsPremises=true OR employsStaff=true. RECOMMENDATION for scope assessment.
Action: “Assess water-system legionella risks and responsibility, implement proportionate controls and monitoring, and review the assessment when circumstances change.”
Sources: HSE [workplace risks](https://www.hse.gov.uk/legionnaires/workplace-risks.htm), [recordkeeping](https://www.hse.gov.uk/legionnaires/what-you-must-do/keeping-records.htm), [landlord responsibilities](https://www.hse.gov.uk/legionnaires/legionella-landlords-responsibilities.htm). Underlying HSWA and COSHH duties; no single legal commencement established here.
Employers/premises controllers must understand/manage risks; a low-risk system may need only a simple proportionate assessment. Do not prescribe universal sampling, a purchased certificate or identical flushing intervals. Five-employee recording rule is distinct from recommended records below that threshold. Retain assessment, responsible person, system/control schedule, monitoring and corrective actions appropriate to actual risk. Cooling systems, pools and specialist systems need specialist review rather than a “domestic kettle” template.

### P3 — Electrical safety

Condition: employsStaff=true. STATUTORY_DUTY with extent-of-control wording; controlsPremises-only should receive a recommendation assessment until relevant dutyholder scope established.
Action: “Maintain electrical systems and equipment under the organisation’s control to prevent danger; arrange competent, risk-based inspection, maintenance and remedial action.”
Sources: [Electricity at Work Regulations 1989 reg4 XML](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/635/regulation/4/data.xml), HSE [PAT FAQs](https://www.hse.gov.uk/electricity/faq-portable-appliance-testing.htm), current page updated2025-04-16.
No universal annual PAT, label, testing-record or fixed EICR interval follows from this source. Fixed installations need their own suitable assessment. Evidence: equipment/installations responsibility, risk-based maintenance schedule, competence, defects isolated and repaired. Records are sensible evidence; distinguish these from a universal legal requirement to keep PAT records. Historical commencement not checked; current text verified.

### P4 — Gas safety

Condition: controlsPremises=true OR employsStaff=true. RECOMMENDATION for inventory/scope, since flags do not establish gas presence or landlord status.
Action: “Identify gas installations and responsible dutyholders; maintain relevant systems safely, use appropriately qualified contractors and fulfil any applicable landlord inspection duties.”
Sources: HSE [gas for employers](https://www.hse.gov.uk/gas/employers.htm), [landlord checks](https://www.hse.gov.uk/gas/landlords/safetycheckswhat.htm); [GSIUR1998 reg35 XML](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/2451/regulation/35/data.xml).
Reg35 concerns employer/self-employed gas appliances, installation pipework and flues at workplaces under their control. Annual landlord checks are a separate conditional regime, not a universal commercial-premises interval. Schools normally fall within GSIUR; industrial exclusions require separate competence analysis. Evidence: gas inventory or documented absence, allocation, service/repair records, contractor registration/competence and landlord checks where applicable. Do not default every institution to a CP12 requirement.

### P5 — Hazardous substances

Condition: employsStaff=true. RECOMMENDATION for screening; substantive assessment/control is statutory where work liable to expose employees exists.
Action: “Identify hazardous substances and exposure-producing work; complete and implement required COSHH assessments, controls, information and review.”
Sources: HSE [COSHH assessment](https://www.hse.gov.uk/coshh/basics/assessment.htm); [COSHH2002 reg6 XML](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/2677/regulation/6/data.xml).
Include cleaning products, generated dust/fumes and biological risks, not just labelled chemicals. SDS alone is not the risk assessment. Eliminate/substitute/control exposure; assess health surveillance, monitoring, emergencies and control-system testing where relevant. Reg6 prohibits exposure work before suitable assessment AND implementation; record significant findings/control steps for five-plus employees. Evidence: inventory/SDS, actual-work assessment, controls/training, review/remediation. Do not convert HSE's LEV testing summary into a universal rule for all room ventilation.

### P6 — Work equipment and lifting scope

Condition: employsStaff=true OR controlsPremises=true. RECOMMENDATION for assessment.
Action: “Identify work equipment and responsible users/controllers; implement suitable guarding, safe use, maintenance, inspection and training, including any separate lifting-equipment examination duties.”
Sources: HSE [PUWER overview](https://www.hse.gov.uk/work-equipment-machinery/puwer.htm); [PUWER1998 reg5 XML](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/2306/regulation/5/data.xml).
Reg5 requires efficient condition/order/repair and updates to an existing machinery maintenance log. PUWER scope includes work use/control, not every toy automatically; inspections depend on installation/deterioration risk. LOLER should be flagged for further actual-equipment assessment; its detailed intervals were not verified in this pass and must not be invented. Evidence: inventory, suitability/guarding assessment, maintenance, inspection/competence records and specialist examination decisions.

## Residential and activity actions

### A1 — Residential classification and applicable standards

Condition: boarding=true (school or any other sector). RECOMMENDATION classification action; replace/extend existing boarding-classification rather than duplicate it.
Action: “Establish the residential provision’s legal route, obtain required registration/approval, and implement the applicable boarding, residential-special-school or children’s-home framework.”
Sources: DfE [boarding landing page](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/boarding-schools-national-minimum-standards), published2012-11-01, updated2026-05-18, [May2026 current PDF](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a0c1d22c75cc34a8ff8f499/National_Minimum_Standards_for_Boarding_Schools.pdf); [residential-special landing page](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/residential-special-schools-national-minimum-standards), published2012-11-01, updated2026-08-06, [current corrected PDF](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a72fb64de77e2943cd3bbd9/National_minimum_standards_for_residential_special_schools_2026.pdf). Edition application dates not separately established; use currentAsOf, not old2015 footnote as new commencement.
NMS are statutory standards under Children Act1989 s87C supporting welfare inspection, not primary legislation nor one universal checklist. Mainstream and special-school routes differ. Current special-school PDF restores standard23 omitted in May; do not use superseded PDF. Map applicable standards and appendices for policies, records, accommodation, welfare, health, staffing, complaints, activities and off-site arrangements; retain inspection findings and completed remedies. The boarding flag alone cannot select the exact statutory route.
[Care Standards Act2000 s1 XML](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/14/section/1/data.xml) was read. School children’s-home test is MORE THAN295 days, not295-or-more. It includes intended accommodation for a child for more than295 days in any12-month period; actual-history limb examines each year in prior two years, including school-arranged accommodation and at least one child meeting the duration. Do not replace this with “school open295days.” [Ofsted registration guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/register-a-childrens-home) supplies registration route. Section1 wholly in force2002-04-01 but amended current scope has multiple dates; currentAsOf preferred. Children’s-home quality standards need their own detailed review if actual route selected; this classification action does not close every children’s-home duty.
Evidence: provision/ages/days and intention assessment, regulator decision/registration, applicable-standard matrix, policies/records and actions. Visiting pupils, off-site lodging and holiday lettings need explicit responsibility allocation.

### A2 — Private fostering and host accommodation

Condition: unconditional RECOMMENDATION screening. Organisations may arrange host-family care without campus boarding or trips; regulation 3(2) applies to involvement in arranging, so neither flag can safely exclude this assessment.
Action: “Assess off-site/host accommodation for private-fostering duties and make required local-authority notifications before or promptly on arrangements.”
Sources: [Children Act1989 s66 XML](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/66/data.xml), [Private Arrangements for Fostering Regulations2005 reg3 XML](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1533/regulation/3/data.xml), [DfE statutory private-fostering guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/children-act-1989-private-fostering).
Under16 (under18 disabled), non-parent/non-parental-responsibility/non-relative home care; statutory exceptions matter. Less-than28days exclusion also requires no intention of longer provision. Thus do not use a “more than28” threshold or wait28days before notification. Proposed carer: at least6weeks beforehand or immediately if sooner; person involved in arranging: as soon as possible after arrangement. Different responsible persons/timescales, not one universal6week rule. Evidence: duration/relationship/exemption assessment, local children’s-services contact and confidential notification receipt; keep child details out of public checklist notes. Section66 original force1991-10-14, later amendments separately; currentAsOf for current text.

### A3 — Adventure activity scope, provider licence and safety

Condition: trips=true (but see limitation below). RECOMMENDATION assessment.
Action: “Assess adventure-activity licensing scope and exemptions; verify the actual provider’s current licence where required and suitable safety arrangements in every case.”
Sources: HSE [scheme legislation](https://www.hse.gov.uk/aala/general-information.htm), [provider/school/club guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/aala/are-you-a.htm), [application](https://www.hse.gov.uk/aala/apply-for-a-licence.htm). Current guidance reflects Adventure Activities Licensing Regulations2004.
Scope depends on specified activity, under18 participants, payment and exemptions. Schools’ own-pupil provision and qualifying voluntary-association arrangements can be exempt; selling to general public/schools or temporary course memberships is different. Parent exemption requires actual parent/legal guardian participation/proximity, not any responsible adult. A licence must exist before licensable provision; application3months ahead is guidance. Evidence: activity/provider/customer assessment, licence register check/date/scope/expiry, competent leadership, risk/emergency arrangements. No blanket activity licence for all school trips.
Limitation: a club may provide licensable activity without calling it a trip. Either make this screening action unconditional across institutional sectors with “if any” assessment, or explicitly keep on-site activity coverage unresolved. Do not assume trips=false proves no adventure provision.

### A4 — Passenger transport operational assessment

Condition: transport=true OR operatesVehicles=true. RECOMMENDATION; extend existing transport action.
Action: “Document the operator, vehicle and service route; obtain the required licence/permit or supported exemption, and verify drivers, insurance, roadworthiness and applicable hours rules.”
Sources: DfT [section19/22 current detailed guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/section-19-and-22-permits-not-for-profit-passenger-transport/section-19-and-22-permits-not-for-profit-passenger-transport), landing published2013-08-07 updated2020-12-30; [driving minibus](https://www.gov.uk/driving-a-minibus), [school minibus advice](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/driving-school-minibuses-advice-for-schools-and-local-authorities/driving-school-minibuses-advice-for-schools-and-local-authorities).
Hire/reward, commercial activity, passenger classes, vehicle size and actual permit eligibility matter. Charity does not automatically exempt; permit does not waive driver entitlement or safe vehicles. Verify outsourcing providers too. Retain operator/permit decision, vehicle entitlement and maintenance, insurer confirmation, driver category/medical/competence and applicable hours/CPC assessment; no uniform fatigue rule across regimes. Minibus licence is not automatic from adult account or basic driving licence. Taxi/private-hire, international operations and specialist vehicle requirements require separately evidenced route review.

## Local permissions and regional inventory

### L1 — Planning and building-control assessment

Condition: controlsPremises=true OR boarding=true; for institutions hiring premises with controlsPremises=false, include under unconditional registration/classification review so responsibility is not lost.
RECOMMENDATION: “Verify lawful use and premises works with the actual planning/building-control authority; obtain required approvals, discharge conditions and retain completion evidence before relevant use or work.”
Sources: GOV.UK [planning permission](https://www.gov.uk/planning-permission-england-wales), [building regulations approval](https://www.gov.uk/building-regulations-approval). Planning/change-of-use and building regulations are different; one approval does not replace the other. The latter identifies Building Regulations2010 as its legal basis. Attempted direct reg5XML did not retrieve successfully; do not claim detailed law/commencement review. A school’s educational registration does not settle permission, permitted development, listed-building, lease, fire or building-control questions.
Evidence: site address and actual LPA, use/works assessment, relevant permissions/lawful-development evidence, conditions register, building-control route/completion and landlord consent where needed. Do not prescribe a planning application for every school or every change.

### L2 — Venue/event licensing

Condition: controlsPremises=true OR charitable=true is insufficient for all events; prefer an unconditional concise activity-screening recommendation, or explicitly retain uncovered events as a gap.
Action: “Screen venue and event activities for licensing, permitted exemptions and temporary-event routes; secure required authorisations and meet their conditions.”
Source: GOV.UK [premises licence](https://www.gov.uk/find-licences/premises-licence), national overview. Alcohol, regulated entertainment and late-night refreshment have different triggers/exemptions; charity is not blanket exemption. The overview is not enough for a universal entertainment licence rule: educational/incidental exceptions and deregulation mean actual activity/hours/audience/premises must be assessed. Detailed Licensing Act schedules/TEN rules not independently reviewed here. Evidence: licensing-authority decision/assessment, permission/notice, named owner and condition/calendar review. Do not import page summary's blanket DPS wording into non-alcohol cases.

### L3 — Food authority responsibilities

Existing food items are substantially supported. [FSA food-business registration](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/food-business-registration), current reviewed2026-10-15, published2023-08-31 updated2026-06-25: keep site-specific registration/approval route and environmental-health authority evidence. National actions can be shared; the actual council registration, approval conditions and findings cannot be inferred from region. Do not duplicate existing items.

### Regional inventory recommendation

[ONS England administrative geography](https://www.ons.gov.uk/methodology/geography/ukgeographies/administrativegeography/england) explains the retained statistical regions. Record North East, North West, Yorkshire and The Humber, East Midlands, West Midlands, East of England, London, South East, South West as geographical inventory only. Their listing does not establish nine separate legal codes or nine reviewed licensing authorities.

For each jurisdiction×sector×topic, distinguish “national route reviewed and applicable in all nine regions” from “actual local rules/permissions unverified.” Retain regionalStatus=IN_REVIEW/UNAVAILABLE until genuine local coverage exists, or introduce an explicit national-route-only status with equally visible local gaps. Do not fill regions[] with nine names and mark PUBLISHED merely to clear the engine warning. That would turn mapping into an unsupported completeness claim.

A site authority register/evidence note should identify actual planning authority, building-control body, licensing/environmental-health authority, fire enforcing authority, children’s services and safeguarding partnership separately. One localAuthority text field is not proof these bodies coincide. Official national council lookup can help navigation; validate the authority for the postcode/activity and record a dated source/decision. Site-specific permissions may be completed by manual evidence without claiming the catalogue has reviewed every council’s policy.

## Remaining publication limits

This pass gives defensible national assessment actions and several precise substantive duties. It does NOT close all specialist regime details: full children’s-home quality standards, residential NMS item-by-item evidence, lifting/pressure systems, construction/client duties, swimming pools, waste/environmental permits, detailed taxi/international transport, all entertainment exemptions, listed buildings and every council condition remain beyond verified detail here. Do not mark an entire jurisdiction×sector×topic fully reviewed just because a generic “obtain specialist advice” action exists. Regional publication must describe the exact research boundary. Source IDs and engine fixtures should test false/unknown flags, own-pupil exemption versus commercial provider, >295 boundary, intended private fostering, and actual legal provider independence from screen/sector.
