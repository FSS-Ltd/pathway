# Finland national checklist pack (2.14)

Reviewed 2026-10-19. Catalogue 1.15.0 adds **13 conditional actions** and **20 official/provider sources** across six institutional sectors and eight topics. All 48 Finland country cells are `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`, with regional status `IN_REVIEW`. A manual completion count is not legal certification.

Formal basic or pre-primary education needs its own provider classification and, for a private mainland provider, authorisation under [Basic Education Act section 7](https://www.finlex.fi/en/legislation/translations/1998/eng/628). A Learning Centre screen, ACE participation or private funding does not establish that status. [The education agency](https://www.oph.fi/en/internationalisation/early-childhood-education-and-pre-primary-education-children-who-have-fled) distinguishes the authorised route; [home education guidance](https://www.oph.fi/en/node/1062) makes classification important for group provision.

The [current Early Childhood Education and Care Act](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2018/540?language=fin) and [Kela's operator guidance](https://www.kela.fi/yhteistyokumppanit-varhaiskasvatuksen-tuottajat-yksityisen-hoidon-tuki-toiminnan-aloittaminen) distinguish 2026 Soteri registration for private daycare centres from municipal notice for private family daycare. Older English permit guidance is not treated as the current route for new operators. The [revised national ECEC grounds](https://www.oph.fi/fi/koulutus-ja-tutkinnot/varhaiskasvatussuunnitelman-perusteet-pahkinankuoressa) and individual child plans remain relevant; the superseded general local ECEC plan duty is excluded. Current [pre-primary and basic-education support changes](https://www.oph.fi/fi/uutiset/2026/esi-ja-perusopetus-kaikki-oppimisen-tuen-uudistukseen-liittyvat-lakimuutokset-astuneet) must be checked for actual authorised provision.

Official [education-personnel reporting guidance](https://www.oph.fi/fi/koulutus-ja-tutkinnot/varhaiskasvatuksen-ja-koulutuksen-henkiloston-ilmoitusvelvollisuudet-ja) and [child-work extract guidance](https://www.suomi.fi/services/criminal-records-extract-for-working-with-children-legal-register-centre/8ac8873e-708f-4d01-b7bb-65e44fccd631) support role-specific checks. [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), [Finnish education privacy guidance](https://www.oph.fi/fi/koulutus-ja-tutkinnot/tietosuoja-kasvatuksessa-opetuksessa-ja-koulutuksessa), [employer risk-assessment guidance](https://www.ttl.fi/teemat/tyoturvallisuus/tyoturvallisuuslain-soveltamisopas/tyonantajan-yleiset-velvollisuudet/10-ss-tyon-vaarojen-selvittaminen-ja-arviointi) and the [Finnish Food Authority's food-business route](https://www.ruokavirasto.fi/en/foodstuffs/food-sector/setting-up-a-food-business/make-an-announcement-of-a-food-business-or-apply-for-an-approval/rekisteroity-elintarviketoiminta/) support conditional operational actions. ICCE terms apply only when qualifications are actually offered.

The [regional review](regional-review.md) records unresolved Åland, mainland municipality and local-authority duties. Site managers must confirm uncertain applicability with the competent authority before marking a review action not applicable. No checkbox is ticked automatically. There is no UI or database migration in this pack.
