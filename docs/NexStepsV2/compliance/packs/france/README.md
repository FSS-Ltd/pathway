# France national checklist pack (2.15)

Reviewed 2026-10-19. Catalogue 1.16.0 adds 17 conditional actions across six institutional sectors and eight topics. All 48 France country cells are `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`, with regional status `IN_REVIEW`. The checklist is a manual record, not legal certification.

Actual provision determines legal status. A Nexsteps School or Learning Centre screen, ACE affiliation and the Public Learning Centre label do not establish French school status or public funding. [Private schools outside a state contract](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F33876) normally follow the [Education Code article L441-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000043983848) rectorate declaration route. [Article L481-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000043983842) retains prior authorisation in Bas-Rhin, Haut-Rhin and Moselle. The catalogue distinguishes these routes by selected operating department and leaves missing department information unresolved.

[Youth-ministry ACM guidance](https://www.jeunes.gouv.fr/organisateurs-ce-qu-il-faut-savoir-sur-les-accueils-collectifs-de-mineurs-217) separates regulated collective-minor activities from ordinary tutoring and identifies declaration and staff duties. Under-six childcare must be classified under the [Public Health Code article L2324-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000048599947) EAJE route; a childcare checkbox alone does not establish an authorisation or specific establishment category. [CNIL education guidance](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/education-pro), [employer risk assessment guidance](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2210) and [ERP safety guidance](https://entreprendre.service-public.fr/vosdroits/F31684) support conditional operating actions. ICCE provider terms apply only when qualifications are offered.

Managers should retain authority decisions and check uncertain classification with the academy, department or competent local authority before marking an action not applicable. See the [regional review](regional-review.md) for unresolved local and overseas scope. No checkbox is ticked automatically.
