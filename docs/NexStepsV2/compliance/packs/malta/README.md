# Malta national institutional checklist, version 1.24.0

Reviewed 2026-10-20. This progressive pack publishes 27 conditional and classification actions with 36 official or provider sources across six institutional sectors and eight topics. All 48 Malta country cells remain `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`; regional-council, local-council and site-specific legal review remains `IN_REVIEW`. Manual confirmation measures checklist completion, not legal certification. The [local-council review](local-council-review.md) documents the six regions and 68 councils used for location selection.

The [Education Act](https://legislation.mt/eli/cap/605/eng) and [DQSE school licensing](https://dqse.gov.mt/en/school-licensing/) distinguish actual Church, independent, international and specialised school routes. A Nexsteps Church sector or ACE type is not a licence. [DQSE ECEC licensing](https://dqse.gov.mt/en/licence/) separately covers 0–3 care: a Planning Authority permit alone does not allow operation. [Non-formal registration](https://dqse.gov.mt/en/application-for-accreditation-non-formal/), [summer-school licensing](https://education.gov.mt/summer-schools/) and [MFHEA Tuition Centre/provider categories](https://mfhea.mt/categories-of-providers-licenses/) need actual course, age and qualification classification. [MFHEA's register and stated exclusions](https://mfhea.mt/list-of-licensed-providers-and-accredited-programmes/) mean an ICCE record alone cannot prove Maltese provider licensing or MQF recognition. Uncertain routes must be checked with DQSE or MFHEA.

The [Protection of Minors (Registration) Act](https://legislation.mt/eli/cap/518/eng) and [DQSE ECEC guidance](https://dqse.gov.mt/en/faqs/more-information/) require role- and setting-specific POMA review. An adult or parent portal account is not itself a child-contact role. [Teacher-warrant rules](https://legislation.mt/eli/cap/606/eng) depend on covered teaching duties. Safeguarding and referral arrangements use the [education safeguarding policy](https://education.gov.mt/publications/safeguarding-policy/) and [child-protection service](https://fsws.gov.mt/child-protection/mandatory-reporting-2/), applied to the actual setting.

[GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) and [Maltese IDPC guidance](https://idpc.org.mt/our-office/legislation/) govern actual processing; DPIA and DPO triggers are conditional. [Workplace safety](https://legislation.mt/eli/cap/646/eng), [food-premises registration](https://agriculture.gov.mt/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/registration-of-food-premises-fsc.pdf), [food-handler rules](https://www.servizz.gov.mt/en/services/web-00020) and [passenger transport](https://www.transport.gov.mt/land/professional-drivers/becoming-a-passenger-transport-operator/passenger-transport-more-than-9-seats-751) depend on the operator and activities. [Voluntary organisation rules](https://cvo.gov.mt/LEGISLATION) apply to actual legal/enrolment status, not charitable branding.

The checklist exposes unknown classification, local conditions and evidence gaps; live portal signals never tick a manual confirmation. N/A decisions require a reason and direction to check doubtful classifications with the competent authority or local council. Country-level publication is not a claim that every council or individual licence condition has been reviewed.
