# Poland TERC voivodeship and gmina review

Reviewed 2026-10-20 against [GUS's official TERC full-file service](https://eteryt.stat.gov.pl/eTeryt/rejestr_teryt/udostepnianie_danych/baza_teryt/uzytkownicy_indywidualni/pobieranie/pliki_pelne.aspx). Its downloaded `TERC_Urzedowy_2026-10-20.zip` has an effective catalogue date of 2026-01-01. The additive migration seeds 16 voivodeships as regions and 2,479 gminas as local authorities. Official TERC numeric codes are retained with a `PL-TERC-` portal prefix. Gmina display names include their type and powiat because identical names can describe different urban and rural municipalities. Each gmina is parented to its voivodeship; its seven-digit TERC code and display label retain the powiat identification.

The official file also has 380 powiats and separate rows for urban/rural parts, Warsaw districts and city delegatures. Those rows are not extra gminas. The portal's existing country/region/local-authority hierarchy represents the gmina as the selectable local site boundary; the relevant powiat is identified from TERC for school and other county-level decisions. This inventory does **not** establish an individual gmina or powiat registration, building permission, sanitary approval, fire decision or education-superintendent opinion. Confirm those with the authority responsible for the actual address and service before recording a permitted N/A.
