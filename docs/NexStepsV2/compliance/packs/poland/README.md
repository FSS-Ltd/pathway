# Poland national institutional checklist, version 1.26.0

Reviewed 2026-10-20. This progressive pack publishes 31 conditional and classification actions with 31 official or provider sources across six institutional sectors and eight topics. All 48 Poland country cells remain `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`; voivodeship, powiat, gmina and site-specific legal review remains `IN_REVIEW`. Manual confirmation measures checklist completion, not legal certification. The [TERC location review](terc-review.md) describes the 16 voivodeships and 2,479 gminas used for site selection.

Actual provider status controls the education route. [Education Law](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1043/text.html) and the [non-public register service](https://epuap.gov.pl/wps/portal/strefa-urzednika/katalog-spraw/opis-uslugi/wpis-szkoly-lub-placowki-niepublicznej-do-ewidencji-prowadzonej-przez-jednostki-samorzadu-terytorialnego) distinguish non-public school registration from [authorization for a privately operated public school](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2024/31/text.html). [Preschool registration](https://www.gov.pl/web/nauka/zakladanie-i-prowadzenie-placowek-wychowania-przedszkolnego) and the [municipal under-three care register](https://www.gov.pl/web/rodzina/lobek-i-klub-dzieciecy) are separate again. Public subsidy does not itself turn a non-public school into a public school. ACE, Church and non-ACE Learning Centre screens prove none of these statuses. A supplementary tuition centre must check claims about compulsory education, childcare and organised holidays with the competent gmina, powiat or education superintendent.

The [under-three care standards regulation](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2024/1882/text.html) became effective on 1 January 2026. Mandatory standards are distinct from optional quality enhancements; [implementation guidance](https://www.gov.pl/web/rodzina/nowoczesne-i-przyjazne-zlobki-nowe-standardy-opieki-wchodza-w-zycie) distinguishes the RKZ-8 path for existing services and RKZ-1 for newly registered ones. The July 2026 [amendment](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/1123/ogl) chiefly enters into force in 2028 and is not treated as a current duty in this pack. Care ratios, qualifications, premises and local decisions must be checked for the actual age group and registered form.

[Child-protection law](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2024/1802/text.html) requires standards for many child-facing education, religious, care, sport and interest-development settings. [Ministry of Justice scope guidance](https://www.gov.pl/web/sprawiedliwosc/kto-ma-obowiazek-wprowadzenia-standardow-ochrony-maloletnich) and [role guidance](https://www.gov.pl/web/sprawiedliwosc/pytania-do-ustawy-o-ochronie-dzieci2) make screening depend on actual child-facing work: an adult account is not itself a clearance trigger, while a parent volunteering in covered care may be. Keep case records restricted. Teacher disciplinary checks have a separate [CROD route](https://www.gov.pl/web/edukacja/centralny-rejestr-orzeczen-dyscyplinarnych).

[GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), [UODO school guidance](https://uodo.gov.pl/file/1384), [PIP occupational-risk guidance](https://www.pip.gov.pl/dla-pracodawcow/niezbednik-pracodawcy/ocena-ryzyka-zawodowego) and [nursery premises guidance](https://www.gov.pl/web/gis/wymagania-dotyczace-opieki-nad-dziecmi-w-wieku-do-lat-3) apply according to actual data processing, employment and site use. Food, transport, school trips and organised child holidays have distinct activity-specific checks; the [2026 school-food rules](https://www.gov.pl/web/gis/szkola---przestrzen-zdrowego-odzywiania-od-1-wrzesnia-zmiany-w-sklepikach-i-stolowkach-szkolnych) took effect on 1 September. ICCE provider terms apply only to actual participants and do not establish Polish school status or qualification recognition.

The checklist leaves uncertain provider and local decisions visible. It asks for evidence, permits reasoned N/A only where appropriate, and directs doubtful cases to the competent authority. Portal signals remain supporting evidence and never tick a manual confirmation. Country-level publication does not mean every local register, building, sanitary, fire or individual licence condition has been reviewed.
