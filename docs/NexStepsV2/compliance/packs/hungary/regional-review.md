# Hungary county and municipal review

Reviewed 2026-10-20. The [Hungarian Central Statistical Office](https://www.ksh.hu/regionalatlas_counties?lang=en) identifies nineteen counties and Budapest as the capital. The [UNECE subdivision table](https://service.unece.org/trade/locode/2023-1%20SubdivisionCodes.htm) supplies their `HU-` codes. The additive migration makes these twenty locations selectable; Budapest is labelled as the capital, not as an ordinary county.

The locations are Baranya (`HU-BA`), Bács-Kiskun (`HU-BK`), Békés (`HU-BE`), Borsod-Abaúj-Zemplén (`HU-BZ`), Budapest (`HU-BU`), Csongrád-Csanád (`HU-CS`), Fejér (`HU-FE`), Győr-Moson-Sopron (`HU-GS`), Hajdú-Bihar (`HU-HB`), Heves (`HU-HE`), Jász-Nagykun-Szolnok (`HU-JN`), Komárom-Esztergom (`HU-KE`), Nógrád (`HU-NO`), Pest (`HU-PE`), Somogy (`HU-SO`), Szabolcs-Szatmár-Bereg (`HU-SZ`), Tolna (`HU-TO`), Vas (`HU-VA`), Veszprém (`HU-VE`) and Zala (`HU-ZA`).

The current pack publishes national entry routes and requests the operating county for school and child-welfare registration. County-by-county decisions, municipal conditions and current adult-training authority implementation have not been published. All sector/topic country cells remain partial and regional review remains `IN_REVIEW`.
