# Sweden 2.31 — national entry core and county inventory

Reviewed 2026-10-20 against the linked Swedish official legal texts, regulator guidance, EU law and ICCE provider pages. This is a **partial** checklist pack, not legal certification. It publishes national entry actions across six sectors and eight topics. Twenty-one NUTS 2024 county selectors are seeded; the [Swedish statistics authority](https://www.scb.se/en/finding-statistics/regional-statistics/regional-divisions/counties-and-municipalities/) identifies 290 municipalities, whose substantive decisions and local rules remain `IN_REVIEW`. County codes do not replace municipal approval or fire/food registration decisions.

## Applicability decisions

- The [Education Act (2010:800)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/skollag-2010800_sfs-2010-800./) separates school, preschool, pedagogical care and other education forms. The [Schools Inspectorate](https://www.skolinspektionen.se/tillstand/ansok/starta-eller-utoka-fristaende-skola/villkor-for-godkannande/) approves actual independent-school operators for specified education and units, while municipalities approve [independent preschools and certain other provision](https://www.skolinspektionen.se/inspektion/stodmaterial-for-kommuner/stodmaterial-vid-provning-av-agare-ledning-och-ekonomiska-forutsattningar/6.provning-av-ekonomiska-forutsattningar). ACE or the portal's school/learning-centre screen establishes neither route. County and municipal classification remains explicit.
- The current [Social Services Act (2025:400)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/socialtjanstlag-2025400_sfs-2025-400/) Chapter 19 Section 1 requires immediate child-concern reports from listed people, including those active under the Education Act and certain professionally run private services involving children; Section 4 encourages everyone else to report. The pack has a statutory education action and a separate non-school category assessment. It does not call every club or parent a mandated reporter. [Education Act Chapter 6 guidance](https://www.skolverket.se/styrning-och-ansvar/regler-och-ansvar/ansvar-i-skolfragor/krankande-behandling-mobbning-och-diskriminering) concerns actual school/preschool activity.
- [Education Act Chapter 2 Sections 31–33](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/skollag-2010800_sfs-2010-800./) require a qualifying register extract for covered education appointments/placements. At presentation it must be no older than six months, and the permitted record is a note that it was shown, **not an uploaded copy**. [Police guidance for school/preschool](https://polisen.se/tjanster-tillstand/belastningsregistret/skola-eller-forskola/) and [different guidance for non-school child activity](https://polisen.se/tjanster-tillstand/belastningsregistret/barn-annan-verksamhet) must not be conflated. The current consolidated Education Act displays provisions that take effect in 2027; this pack uses the current-effective Chapter 2 route only. [Teacher eligibility](https://www.skolverket.se/kompetensutveckling/legitimation/regler-och-krav-for-legitimation) is assessed by actual role and school form.
- [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) follows actual controller processing. The [Swedish privacy authority's school guidance](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/dataskydd-pa-olika-omraden/skola-och-forskola/) treats child data as especially sensitive; [DPO appointment](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/det-har-galler-enligt-gdpr/dataskyddsombud/) and DPIAs remain conditional. Portal medical records retain restricted access.
- [Work Environment Authority guidance](https://www.av.se/arbetsmiljoarbete-och-inspektioner/arbeta-med-arbetsmiljon/systematiskt-arbetsmiljoarbete/riskbedomning/) requires documented employer risk assessment. [Civil Protection Act Chapter 2 Section 2](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-2003778-om-skydd-mot-olyckor_sfs-2003-778/) requires reasonable fire precautions from building owners or users. Detailed measures depend on site risk and municipal fire oversight.
- [Preschool guidance](https://www.skolverket.se/undervisning/forskolan/laroplan-lpfo-18-for-forskolan/forskolan-styrs-av-skollagen-och-laroplanen) and the [compulsory-school curriculum](https://www.skolverket.se/undervisning/grundskolan/laroplan-lgr22-for-grundskolan-samt-for-forskoleklassen-och-fritidshemmet/curriculums/LGR22/GRGRTSP01?hasSubjects=true&schoolType=GR&tosHeading=Kursplaner) apply to their actual school forms; specialist curricula, pupil-health, group-size and municipal care routes need further review.
- [Municipal food-establishment registration guidance](https://verksamt.se/bransch/hitta-tillstand/tillstand/food-businesses-UKR2055) and [EU Regulation 852/2004](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2004/852/oj/eng) follow actual food-business activity. Occasional events, transport, trips and residential provision require separate classification.
- [Tax Agency association guidance](https://www4.skatteverket.se/rattsligvagledning/edition/2026.2/1726.html) states there is no universal association registration for legal capacity. [Religious-community registration](https://www.kammarkollegiet.se/vara-tjanster/trossamfund/ansok-om-att-registrera-ett-trossamfund) is a separate actual legal-form decision. Church/Charity portal sectors do not make either status automatic. Detailed tax and finance duties remain outside this version.
- [ICCE registration](https://www.icce.education/school-hea-registration), [enrolment](https://www.icce.education/enrolling-students) and [moderation](https://www.icce.education/moderation-1) apply only to actual ICCE participation. They do not grant Swedish school approval or qualification recognition.

The twenty-one codes come from [Eurostat GISCO NUTS 2024](https://gisco-services.ec.europa.eu/distribution/v2/nuts/csv/NUTS_AT_2024.csv). Missing county is `UNAVAILABLE`, never silently Stockholm. The source catalogue records authority, URL, provision, territory, classification, language and review date, with an explicit warning about consolidated-text amendment dates. Site operators should verify doubtful classification, role checks and local rules with their municipality or competent regulator before marking actions complete or not applicable.
