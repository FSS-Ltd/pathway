# Lithuania county and municipality review

Reviewed 2026-10-20. The [State Data Agency municipality layer](https://osp-sdg.stat.gov.lt/arcgis/rest/services/Hosted/Municipalities/FeatureServer/0) provides 60 unique municipality codes and 10 county codes, consistent with its [territorial statistics overview](https://osp.stat.gov.lt/en/regionine-statistika-pagal-statistikos-sritis). Migration `20261111000000_compliance_lithuania_local_governments` adds `LT-APS-XX` county regions and `LT-SAV-XX` municipalities, each municipality parented to its county. County and municipality selection identifies the site; it does not mean local rules have been reviewed.

The [Ministry's first-wave notice](https://smsm.lrv.lt/lt/naujienos-1/pranesimai-ziniasklaidai-1/priemimas-i-mokyklas-ir-darzelius-per-viena-sistema-24-savivaldybese-2p1D/) names the 24 municipalities using CPIS for 2026/27 school and preschool admissions. The codes in the catalogue match the official municipality layer: Akmenė 32, Anykščiai 34, Biržai 36, Druskininkai 15, Elektrėnai 42, Joniškis 47, Jurbarkas 94, Kelmė 54, Kupiškis 57, Lazdijai 59, Molėtai 62, Palanga 25, Panevėžys district 66, Pasvalys 67, Prienai 69, Pakruojis 65, Raseiniai 72, Rokiškis 73, Šakiai 84, Šilalė 87, Švenčionys 86, Varėna 38, Trakai 79 and Tauragė 77. Institution participation and current local operating rules still need confirmation with each municipality. The [non-formal education guidance](https://smsm.lrv.lt/lt/veiklos-sritys-1/smm-svietimas/svietimas-neformalusis/) distinguishes programme accreditation and financing from provider classification.

The follow-up audit must review all 60 municipalities' preschool admission rules, non-formal programme accreditation and financing, child-protection referral contacts, relevant event/camp rules and site-specific decisions. It must confirm the current operative text and commencement of amendments where official consolidated pages do not expose those details clearly. Coverage stays `IN_REVIEW` until that work is complete; an absent local source is not evidence that no obligation exists.
