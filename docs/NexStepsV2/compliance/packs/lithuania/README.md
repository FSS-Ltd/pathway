# Lithuania national institutional checklist, version 1.22.0

Reviewed 2026-10-20. This progressive national pack publishes 32 conditional actions with 33 official or provider sources across all six institutional research sectors and eight topics. Every Lithuania coverage cell is `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`; municipality and site-specific review remains `IN_REVIEW`. Manual confirmation is checklist progress, not legal certification.

Actual provision controls the route. The [current Education Law](https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.1480/BRWWVYIaNs) distinguishes formal education, preschool and non-formal provision. The [Ministry's non-formal guidance](https://smsm.lrv.lt/lt/veiklos-sritys-1/smm-svietimas/svietimas-neformalusis/) identifies schools, other providers and free teachers. The [current ŠMIR register instructions](https://www.smir.smm.lt/aikos2-smir/) require actual non-formal child and adult education providers to register the provider in ŠMIR and the programme in NŠPR; municipal accreditation and public financing are separate decisions. A Nexsteps School or Learning Centre vertical does not establish Lithuanian school status or public funding. The [Ministry's foreign-programme service](https://smsm.lrv.lt/lt/paslaugos/uzsienio-valstybiu-ir-tarptautiniu-organizaciju-svietimo-programu-vykdymas-lietuvoje/) requires a separate assessment when actual ICCE or another international programme is offered; ICCE registration alone is neither Lithuanian consent nor qualification recognition.

The [Child Rights Law](https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/rs/actualedition/TAIS.26397/vquGsHntfJ/format/ISO_PDF/) and [Ministry child-work guidance](https://smsm.lrv.lt/lt/veiklos-sritys-1/smm-svietimas/prevencija-mokyklose/teiseto-darbo-su-vaikais-qr-kodas/) attach QR-code verification to covered child-serving institutions or direct and regular child-contact roles. Covered people are rechecked at least once each calendar year; this is not a certificate expiry date or a requirement for all adult portal accounts. [Order V-774](https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalActEditions/lt/TAD/7367f7d02fbf11e4b487eaabe28831e8) requires teacher qualifications to be assessed by actual programme and role, including any verified exception.

Schools follow [HN 21](https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/rs/actualedition/TAIS.404809/HrOKlxCcEU/format/ISO_PDF/) and the [National Public Health Centre's passport guidance](https://nvsc.lrv.lt/lt/naujienos/nvsc-svarbu-kad-vaikai-sugriztu-i-saugias-mokyklas/); preschool follows [HN 75](https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/rs/actualedition/TAIS.371081/zLZqyxjuHQ/). Qualifying camps follow [HN 79](https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.380773), while the [Centre's camp guidance](https://nvsc.lrv.lt/lt/naujienos/vaiku-poilsio-stovykla-kaip-uztikrinti-saugu-laisvalaiki/) says a separate camp hygiene passport is not issued. Ordinary trips are classified separately. GDPR, work, fire, food and transport actions depend on the site's actual controller, employer, premises or activity status.

The [municipal review](municipal-review.md) records 10 counties and 60 municipalities from the State Data Agency. National guidance says the preschool central-admission system began in 24 municipalities in March 2026, with remaining rollouts planned for 2027–2028; this pack does not presume a specific municipality is already live. Local rules and individual site decisions remain to be checked with the competent authority. Missing municipality information remains unresolved, and portal signals do not complete checklist actions.
