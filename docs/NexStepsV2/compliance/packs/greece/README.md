# Greece national checklist pack (2.17)

Reviewed 2026-10-20. Catalogue 1.18.0 adds **22 conditional actions** and **24 official/provider sources** across six institutional sectors and eight topics. All 48 Greece country cells are `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`, with regional status `IN_REVIEW`. The manual completion count is not legal certification.

Actual provision, rather than a Nexsteps screen or ACE label, controls the route. The [education ministry's private-school law index](https://www.minedu.gov.gr/en/protovathmia-defterovathmia/nomothesia-idiotiki-ekpaidefsi) and [private-school licence procedure](https://eugo.gov.gr/en/services/309920) differ from [tuition/language-centre notification](https://eugo.gov.gr/en/services/417484) and the [2026 teacher commencement procedure](https://eugo.gov.gr/en/services/881208). Missing operating region leaves school and tuition licensing actions unresolved. A Public Learning Centre is not assumed to be publicly funded or legally a school.

For care, [preschool units](https://eugo.gov.gr/en/services/743179) use a municipal establishment/operation licence, while [children's creative-activity centres](https://eugo.gov.gr/en/services/124032) use a regional KDAP route. [Law 4837/2021](https://prosopikosvoithos.gov.gr/file/public/601200d0-bbbd-ec11-887a-6045bd8e2e35) and the [national reporting authority's entity list](https://mila.gov.gr/%CF%86%CE%BF%CF%81%CE%B5%CE%AF%CF%82/) cover named child-protection providers, including preschool care, KDAP and camps. This pack does not impose their officer or criminal-record requirements solely because someone has a school, adult or parent account. Ambiguous exclusions require a recorded reason and authority confirmation.

The [regional review](regional-review.md) records the thirteen regions and Mount Athos. Municipal and regional licence conditions, school-specific protection requirements, curriculum, staffing ratios, fire and food permissions remain in review. ICCE terms apply only where qualifications are offered. No checkbox is ticked automatically.
