# Estonia county and local-authority review

[Statistics Estonia](https://stat.ee/en/find-statistics/statistics-by-region) publishes regional, county and municipality statistics. Estonia has 15 counties and 79 local governments after the administrative reform; current local-government boundaries and competent offices must be checked for each site. County identity alone does not select municipal school, childcare, building, food or child-protection rules.

| Inventory                                                                        | Review status                  | Follow-up                                                                                               |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 15 counties                                                                      | In review                      | Confirm current county and municipality for every operating site.                                       |
| 79 local governments                                                             | In review                      | Check school, preschool and childcare permissions, admissions, premises and child-protection contacts.  |
| Education ministry and EHIS                                                      | National route partly reviewed | Validate each legal institution type, curriculum, activity licence and transitional childcare decision. |
| Agriculture and Food Board, Labour Inspectorate and Data Protection Inspectorate | National route partly reviewed | Confirm local office, actual operator and activity-specific conditions.                                 |

No county or municipality is marked fully reviewed. The tracker must retain the regional gap and must not interpret missing location information as no obligations. Review the [2025 Early Childhood Education Act](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/en/tolge/pdf/503092025001) transition against any existing childcare licence; do not rely on superseded pre-2025 guidance.
