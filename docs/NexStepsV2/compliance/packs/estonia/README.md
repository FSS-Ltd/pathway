# Estonia national checklist pack (2.13)

Reviewed 2026-10-19. Catalogue 1.14.0 adds **16 actions** and **18 official/provider sources** across six institutional sectors and eight topics. All 48 Estonia cells remain `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED` with regional status `IN_REVIEW`. Manual checklist completion is not legal certification.

The [education ministry](https://hm.ee/uldharidus-ja-noored/opetaja-koolijuht-ja-kooli-pidaja/koolitus-voi-tegevusluba) separates private-school activity licences, municipal education permits and hobby-school registration. The [Early Childhood Education Act](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/en/tolge/pdf/503092025001), effective 2025-09-01, distinguishes preschools from childcare institutions; [ministry transition guidance](https://hm.ee/uldharidus-ja-noored/alus-pohi-ja-keskharidus/alusharidus) distinguishes education childcare from social-care services. An ACE or Learning Centre screen set cannot establish legal school status, care subtype or funding.

The [Child Protection Act](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/en/akt/503022025002) and [Social Insurance Board reporting guidance](https://www.sotsiaalkindlustusamet.ee/lapse-heaolu-hindamise-kasiraamat/teade-abivajavast-ja-hadaohus-olevast-lapsest) support reporting and role-relevant child-work restriction actions; an adult account alone is not a child-facing role. [Estonian school privacy guidance](https://www.aki.ee/isikuandmete-tootlemine-koolis), [EU GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), [workplace risk-assessment guidance](https://www.tooelu.ee/en/79/risk-assessment) and [food-authority catering guidance](https://pta.agri.ee/en/food/retail-trade-and-catering) support conditional actions. ICCE terms apply only where qualifications are actually offered.

The [county and municipal inventory](regional-review.md) records local authority gaps. Municipal school and childcare conditions, staff qualifications, care ratios, premises, trips and specialist food routes require further authority review. Site managers should confirm uncertain applicability with the competent authority before marking a review action not applicable. No automatic checkbox completion, UI change or database migration is introduced.
