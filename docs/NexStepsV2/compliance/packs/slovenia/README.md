# Slovenia institutional checklist, version 1.30.0

This is a **partial** national and statistical-region release for all six institutional sectors and eight tracker topics. It is a manual planning checklist, not legal certification. The operator, actual school or childcare route, staff roles, activities and site decide applicability. An ACE or Public Learning Centre screen does not prove school status, a concession or public funding.

## Reviewed entry points

| Topic              | Source and applicability decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Registration       | [ZOFVI](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO445) and the [ministry register route](https://spot.gov.si/en/activities-and-professions/permits-and-declarations/entry-in-the-register-of-providers-of-publicly-valid-education-and-training-programmes?activity=1493) apply to actual providers of publicly valid programmes. [ZVrt](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO447) and [home-care guidance](https://www.gov.si/teme/vzgoja-in-varstvo-na-domu/) distinguish kindergarten, private educator and registered home-carer routes. A Learning Centre needs its own provision assessment.                |
| Safeguarding       | [ZPND Article 6](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO5084) and the [Family Code](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO7556) have different notification triggers and covered bodies for violence or child endangerment. The [education ministry](https://www.gov.si/novice/2023-12-06-usposabljanje-za-vodstvene-in-strokovne-delavce-osnovnih-sol-o-obravnavi-zrtev-spolnih-zlorab/) publishes school-specific protocols. Restricted case facts must stay in safeguarding records.                                                                                                                         |
| Personnel          | [ZOFVI](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO445) and [government teacher guidance](https://spot.gov.si/en/activities-and-professions/professions-and-professional-staff/foreign-teacher) set conditions for covered professional roles, including qualifications, examinations, language and integrity. [Criminal-record guidance](https://e-uprava.gov.si/en/podrocja/personal-documents-certificates-change-of-residence/certificates-and-extracts/certificate-from-the-criminal-records.html?lang=en) describes certificate routes. These conditions do not attach to every adult or parent portal account. |
| Privacy            | [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) and [ZVOP-2](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?sop=2022-01-4187) require controller-specific processing controls. DPIA and DPO triggers require an actual assessment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Premises           | [ZVZD-1](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO5537) requires a written employer safety statement and risk assessment. [Fire and evacuation guidance](https://www.gov.si/teme/pozar/) describes site precautions and conditional formal-plan and training thresholds; building classification remains site-specific.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Education and care | The [Primary School Act](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO448) governs actual basic-school provision, while the [Kindergarten Act](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO447) governs covered preschool routes. Programme, capacity, staffing, admissions and parent-information decisions require the actual approval and municipality.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Activities         | Actual food-business activity follows [Regulation 852/2004](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2004/852/oj/eng) and the [Slovenian establishment route](https://spot.gov.si/en/activities-and-professions/permits-and-declarations/registration-of-food-processing-facilities); the latter distinguishes organised activity from occasional events. Trips, transport and residential activities need separate fact-specific review.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ACE/ICCE           | [ICCE registration](https://www.icce.education/school-hea-registration), [enrolment](https://www.icce.education/enrolling-students) and [moderation](https://www.icce.education/moderation-1) apply only where ICCE qualifications are actually offered. They do not establish Slovenian school or qualification recognition.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

## Territory and remaining review

The additive migration seeds twelve statistical-region selectors from [Eurostat NUTS 2024](https://gisco-services.ec.europa.eu/distribution/v2/nuts/csv/NUTS_AT_2024.csv). They are territorial identifiers, not licensing authorities. The [Slovenian government](https://www.gov.si/en/topics/municipalities-in-numbers/) and [statistical office](https://www.stat.si/statweb/en/News/Index/14129) identify 212 municipalities. Municipality selectors and municipal decisions have not yet been reviewed or seeded. All national cells are `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`; only regional registration cells contain a reviewed authority-check action, while other regional cells remain `IN_REVIEW`.

For full coverage, review the current municipal inventory and all applicable local decisions; programme-specific rules for school levels, minority-language areas, preschool capacities and needs, non-school child-facing roles, premises, food, trips, transport, boarding and camps, and church or charity legal-form duties. Confirm uncertain exceptions with the competent ministry, municipality or regulator before recording N/A. The final programme-wide audit must recheck current source versions and every conditional rule.
