# Latvia national institutional checklist, version 1.21.0

Reviewed 2026-10-20. This progressive national pack publishes 28 conditional actions with 24 official or provider sources across all six institutional research sectors and eight topics. Every Latvia coverage cell is `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`; municipal and site-specific review remains `IN_REVIEW`. Manual confirmation is checklist progress, not legal certification.

Actual provision controls the route. The [Education Quality State Service](https://www.ikvd.gov.lv/lv/izglitibas-iestazu-registracija) distinguishes registered formal institutions from interest education and adult non-formal education that may use a municipal licence or permission. The [Education Law](https://likumi.lv/ta/id/50759), [General Education Law](https://likumi.lv/ta/id/20243), and [2026/2027 licensing notice](https://www.ikvd.gov.lv/lv/jaunums/2027-gada-mainisies-visparejas-izglitibas-un-profesionalas-izglitibas-iestazu-akreditacijas-proceduras-un-termini) set separate programme and accreditation routes. A Nexsteps School or Learning Centre vertical cannot grant either status or public funding.

The [Child Protection Law](https://likumi.lv/ta/id/49096) and [Regulation 474](https://likumi.lv/ta/id/344779) govern covered child roles and institution safety. Section 72's Punishment Register request and annual re-verification attach to qualifying duties, including covered volunteers and service providers, rather than all adult accounts. [Regulation 404](https://likumi.lv/ta/id/258873) defines a separate child-supervision register. [Regulation 890](https://likumi.lv/ta/id/260057) addresses preschool and child-supervision hygiene. The operative Latvian texts were used because some English translations lag amendments.

The [camp regulation](https://likumi.lv/ta/id/197039) has its own day/overnight definitions, advance registration, conditional site opinions, food and municipality notifications; ordinary trips are first classified rather than assumed to be camps. GDPR, the [Data State Inspectorate's school guidance](https://www.dvi.gov.lv/lv/jaunums/dviskaidro-skolenu-personas-datu-apstrade-izglitibas-iestade), [workplace protection](https://likumi.lv/ta/id/26020), [fire safety](https://likumi.lv/ta/id/281646), and food-business rules are tied to actual controller, employer, premises and food activities. ICCE requirements apply only to participating providers and do not imply Latvian recognition.

The [municipal review](municipal-review.md) records 42 current local-government locations from the Central Statistical Bureau's ATVK classifier. Municipal licensing provisions, site-specific conditions, and local referral routes still need source-by-source review. Missing local information remains unresolved, and no checklist item is completed by a portal signal.
