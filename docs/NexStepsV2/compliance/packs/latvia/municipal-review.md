# Latvia local-government review

Reviewed 2026-10-20. The [Central Statistical Bureau's ATVK dataset](https://data.gov.lv/dati/eng/dataset/atvk), including changes effective 22 August 2026, supplies 42 current top-level local governments: 35 municipalities and seven state cities. The additive migration uses the official `LV-ATVK-<code>` identifiers and stores all 42 as local authorities directly under Latvia. Varakļāni is no longer a separate current local government; the current Madona code is `0038001`. Rīga is `0001000`.

The [Education Quality State Service](https://www.ikvd.gov.lv/lv/izglitibas-programma) identifies municipal permission or licensing for interest education and some adult non-formal provision outside registered education institutions. The [camp regulation](https://likumi.lv/ta/id/197039) requires notice to the municipality where a covered camp takes place. Provider classifications, municipal binding rules and individual site decisions have not been reviewed for every authority. Their coverage therefore remains `IN_REVIEW`; a selected authority does not imply its local duties are fully enumerated.

The follow-up audit must inspect each current municipality's interest-education licensing rules, relevant preschool or child-supervision implementation, event and food permissions, and child-protection contact route. It must also distinguish state-city and municipality decisions where a provider operates in more than one territory. Source updates, legal commencement and effective dates need re-verification before any cell can become fully published.
