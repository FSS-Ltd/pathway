# Northern Ireland district inventory in progress

Checked 20 October 2026. This is a territorial inventory, not a review of
individual council policies, permissions, licences, food controls or local
safeguarding arrangements. The national checklist remains partially published;
every new district sector/topic cell remains `IN_REVIEW`.

The [Northern Ireland government](https://www.nidirect.gov.uk/articles/local-councils)
and [ONS administrative geography guidance](https://www.ons.gov.uk/methodology/geography/ukgeographies/administrativegeography/northernireland)
identify **11 local government districts**, replacing the former 26 areas in 2015. The [ONS April 2025 LAD names-and-codes
file](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/b2c91962-58e7-40f1-ad56-7aa2473a93fd/local-authority-districts-april-2025-names-and-codes-in-the-uk-v2)
contains exactly 11 `N09` district codes. Those codes and names are the source
for the catalogue and additive migration; they must match the database after
deployment.

Districts are not education or child-protection authorities for every duty.
The [Education Authority](https://www.eani.org.uk/about-us/what-we-do) is a
regional body for education and youth services. The [Department of Education's
safeguarding guidance](https://www.education-ni.gov.uk/articles/child-protection-and-safeguarding-education)
also identifies Health and Social Care Trusts, police and other partners.
The [Department of Health safeguarding framework](https://www.health-ni.gov.uk/publications/co-operating-safeguard-children-and-young-people-northern-ireland)
covers statutory, private, voluntary, community and faith settings. A council
selection cannot determine the site's HSC Trust, registration route or actual
safeguarding referral route without further verified location and provision
data.

District councils do matter for some activities: [nidirect](https://www.nidirect.gov.uk/articles/find-your-local-council)
identifies local planning and environmental-health services. Food registration
and any premises or activity licence must be checked with the competent body
for the site's actual operation. These selectors publish no new legal duty.
The next research pass must review each district's applicable controls and the
separate HSC Trust and specialist geography before any local cell is marked
published. Missing district information remains unresolved in the tracker.
