# Northern Ireland national checklist pack (2.04)

Reviewed 2026-10-18. Catalogue 1.5.0 publishes a **partial national core** for six institutional sectors and eight topics. The machine-readable actions and 27 source records are in `apps/api/src/compliance/catalogue/northern-ireland/`. Source classification, applicability, expected evidence and review date are recorded per item. This is a checklist aid, not legal certification.

The [Department of Education's independent-school route](https://www.education-ni.gov.uk/articles/independent-schools-registration) requires registration of actual independent schools and describes applying within one month of opening, provisional registration, inspection, annual information and change notifications. A selected ACE or Learning Centre screen does not establish that status. [Department of Education safeguarding guidance](https://www.education-ni.gov.uk/articles/publications-and-guidance-child-protection-issues-schools) and [Circular 2025/06](https://www.education-ni.gov.uk/publications/circular-202506-pre-employment-accessni-check-requirements-and-safer-recruitment-practices-staff-and-volunteers-working-or-providing-service-schoolseducation-settings) cover schools and role-based recruitment. [Department of Justice AccessNI guidance](https://www.justice-ni.gov.uk/articles/regulated-activity-and-enhanced-checks) describes the September 2026 change to supervised child-facing regulated activity. An adult portal account alone is not a regulated role. [HSC Trust childcare registration](https://www.health-ni.gov.uk/articles/family-policy) and [minimum standards](https://www.health-ni.gov.uk/publications/care-standards-documents) require actual service classification. Northern Ireland uses the [2006 fire Order](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2006/1254/contents), its own [2026/27 attendance guidance](https://www.education-ni.gov.uk/publications/attendance-guidance-2627-academic-year-including-changes-use-codes), and the operative [SEN code](https://www.education-ni.gov.uk/articles/special-educational-needs-code-practice); draft replacements are not treated as current law.

All regional cells remain `IN_REVIEW`. The actual HSC Trust, district council, local safeguarding route, provider contract, school classification and specialist licensing must be confirmed for each site. Curriculum, childcare ratios, residential provision, data rights/DPIA/DPO, food approvals and local transport licensing are incomplete. ICCE items are provider requirements only for actual ICCE participants. A missing fact stays unresolved rather than selecting England or silently claiming no obligation.

All official/provider source pages were retrievable in the research browser. Automated HTTP checks returned 200 for 26 of 27 records; the Food Standards Agency's [Safe Catering PDF](https://www.food.gov.uk/sites/default/files/media/document/safe-catering_0.pdf) returned an HTTP access error to the script but its content was available through source search. See [PROGRESS.md](../../PROGRESS.md) for check and PR evidence.
