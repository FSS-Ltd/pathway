# Spain 2.30 — national entry core and territorial inventory

Reviewed 2026-10-20 against the linked official consolidated texts, EU law and ICCE provider pages. This is a **partial** checklist pack, not a declaration that a Spanish site complies with all applicable law. The current catalogue publishes national entry actions across six sectors and eight topics. It seeds the 17 autonomous communities plus Ceuta and Melilla as territorial selectors. All substantive autonomous-community and municipal rules remain `IN_REVIEW`; the regional registration action asks the site to confirm its competent authority and actual decisions.

## Applicability decisions

- [Organic Law 2/2006 on Education](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-7899) distinguishes public, private and publicly funded private centres. [Royal Decree 332/1992](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1992-7937) addresses private-school authorisation. A portal sector, ACE membership, funding choice or learning-centre screen is not an authorisation. The actual provision, school level, operator and competent education administration determine the route. Autonomous-community and Ceuta/Melilla allocation must be confirmed, rather than assuming one national licensing office.
- [Organic Law 8/2021](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2021-9347) Articles 15–16 frame reporting; Article 35 covers school welfare/protection coordinators; Article 48 covers entities habitually conducting sport or leisure with minors; Article 57 concerns covered roles with habitual contact with minors. The current profile does not distinguish habitual sport/leisure activity or individual worker roles. The pack therefore does **not** apply Article 48 as an unconditional club duty or demand a certificate from every adult portal user. Role decisions remain explicit and must be checked with the competent authority.
- [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) follows the actual controller and processing. [Organic Law 3/2018](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-16673) Article 34 separately requires a data-protection officer for education centres at covered levels. DPIA screening remains conditional. Portal medical data requires its existing restricted access boundaries.
- [Royal Decree 95/2022](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-1654) provides an early-childhood education framework; autonomous communities set significant curriculum and first-cycle details. [Royal Decree 132/2010](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2010-4132) addresses minimum requirements for second-cycle infant, primary and secondary education, **not** every childcare or informal learning provider.
- [Law 31/1995](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1995-24292) follows actual employer operations. [Royal Decree 314/2006](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-5515) building obligations depend on site use and works. [Regulation 852/2004](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2004/852/oj/eng) follows actual food-business activity; [Law 17/2011](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2011-11604) has school-food provisions. The newer school-meal decree, food-registration details and local fire thresholds require a separate effective-date and applicability review before adding a specific action.
- Actual legal form determines whether [association](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-5852), [foundation](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-25180) or [religious-entity](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-8643) rules apply. Church/Charity UI labels do not decide this. Detailed tax and financial filings remain outside this version.
- [ICCE registration](https://www.icce.education/school-hea-registration), [enrolment](https://www.icce.education/enrolling-students) and [moderation](https://www.icce.education/moderation-1) are provider requirements only when ICCE qualifications are actually offered. They do not grant Spanish school status or qualification recognition.

## Territorial and source limits

The nineteen codes come from [Eurostat GISCO NUTS 2024 attributes](https://gisco-services.ec.europa.eu/distribution/v2/nuts/csv/NUTS_AT_2024.csv). They are geographical selectors, not licensing jurisdictions. Eurostat's `ES52` label contains trailing whitespace; the migration deliberately trims it to `Comunitat Valenciana`. The applicable autonomous-community acts, provincial/municipal decisions, specialist provision, ratios, local safeguarding referrals, food registration, transport, boarding and activity rules remain unreviewed. Missing region is `UNAVAILABLE`, never silently resolved to Madrid or another default.

The source records store URL, issuing authority, provision, territorial scope, classification, language, retrieval/review dates and an explicit consolidated-text limitation. English action descriptions are working summaries of Spanish originals. Before a site marks an action complete or not applicable, it should check uncertain classification and local rules with its competent authority; this checklist is not legal certification.
