# Scotland council-area inventory in progress

Checked 20 October 2026. This is a territorial inventory, not a review of each
council's policies, registration decisions, licences or child-protection
arrangements. The Scotland national checklist remains partially published; all
new council-area sector/topic cells remain `IN_REVIEW`.

The [Scottish Government](https://www.gov.scot/policies/local-government/)
states that Scotland has **32 local authorities** and identifies education and
social care among their services. [Education Scotland](https://education.gov.scot/about-education-scotland/local-authority-role-and-duties/)
also identifies 32 education authorities. The [ONS April 2025 county and unitary
authority names-and-codes file](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/1c611b98-7872-47ea-a3f6-71488ab22374/county-and-unitary-authority-april-2025-names-and-codes-in-the-uk)
contains exactly 32 `S12` Scottish council-area codes. Its codes and names were
compared with the generated catalogue and additive migration before publication.

Do not use the [ONS January 2025 LAU level 1
file](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/37a1a24b-5934-4617-beae-ebd90f7793cd/local-administrative-units-level-1-january-2025-names-and-codes-in-the-uk)
as a council list: it has 33 Scottish statistical units because it splits North
Ayrshire mainland from Arran and Cumbrae. North Ayrshire is one council in the
legal council-area inventory. Statistical units do not create an extra authority.

Council boundaries do not identify every competent regulator. [Scottish
independent-school registration guidance](https://www.gov.scot/publications/registration-independent-schools-scotland-guidance-applicants-proprietors-parents/)
describes the Scottish Ministers' registration role, which is distinct from a
council's education service. The [national child-protection
guidance](https://www.gov.scot/publications/national-guidance-child-protection-scotland-2021-updated-2023/pages/5/)
describes Child Protection Committees in each local authority and collaboration
where police or health boundaries span council areas. Childcare, fire, health,
food and specialist activity decisions may involve other authorities. For each
actual provision, verify the competent authority and its current procedure.

Catalogue 1.34.0 and the additive migration seed the 32 council-area selectors
directly beneath Scotland, using ONS codes. Selecting a council displays its
unreviewed topic cells alongside Scotland's national actions. It does not publish
council-specific duties merely because the council can be selected. Review local
safeguarding procedures, education and care classification, registration,
premises, food, transport and other applicable activity routes before closing a
cell. Missing council information continues to show unresolved regional scope.
