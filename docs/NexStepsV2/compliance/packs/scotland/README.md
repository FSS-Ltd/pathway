# Scotland national checklist pack (2.03)

Reviewed 2026-10-18. Catalogue 1.4.0 publishes a **partial national core** for all six institutional sectors and eight topics. The machine-readable records in `apps/api/src/compliance/catalogue/scotland/` carry each action's classification, source, expected evidence, applicability condition and review date. All 31 source URLs returned HTTP 200 during review. This is checklist research, not a legal certification.

The [Scottish Government's independent-school registration guidance](https://www.gov.scot/publications/registration-independent-schools-scotland-guidance-applicants-proprietors-parents/) governs actual school status; an ACE-branded Learning Centre is not automatically a registered school. The [Care Inspectorate](https://www.careinspectorate.com/index.php/register-a-care-service) has a distinct route for regulated childcare and school care accommodation. [Disclosure Scotland](https://www.disclosure.gov.scot/news/2026/09/understanding-your-legal-responsibilities-under-the-pvg-scheme) limits mandatory PVG membership to regulated roles. The [2026 attendance guidance](https://www.gov.scot/publications/included-engaged-involved-part-1-improving-attendance-scotlands-schools/) is non-statutory; the [2017 additional-support code](https://www.gov.scot/publications/supporting-childrens-learning-statutory-guidance-education-additional-support-learning-scotland/) remains the operative edition rather than the draft 2026 fourth edition. The pack uses [Scottish non-domestic fire guidance](https://www.gov.scot/policies/fire-and-rescue/non-domestic-fire-safety/) rather than England's fire order.

All regional cells remain `IN_REVIEW`. A site must establish its council and Child Protection Committee procedures, school or care classification, responsible authority, contract terms and applicable specialist duties locally. The national pack does not resolve charity-specific, care workforce, curriculum, ratios, building, data-rights or local-licensing detail. Missing facts remain unresolved in the tracker rather than selecting England or treating an unreviewed area as having no duty. ICCE actions are provider requirements only when ICCE is actually offered.

Verification includes schema validation, six-sector/eight-topic coverage assertions, independent-school plus ICCE and non-ACE centre applicability fixtures, preservation of prior catalogue history, API type/lint/build, and the relevant tracker unit and integration checks. See [PROGRESS.md](../../PROGRESS.md) for exact run and PR evidence.
