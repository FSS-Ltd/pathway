# Austria national checklist pack (2.06)

Reviewed 2026-10-18. Catalogue 1.7.0 publishes a **partial national core** for six institutional sectors and eight topics. The 16 actions and 16 official/provider source records are in `apps/api/src/compliance/catalogue/austria/`. German source summaries are identified as working translations. Completion is a manual checklist measure, not legal certification.

[Privatschulgesetz § 2](https://ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10009266&Paragraf=2&FassungVom=2026-10-18) defines a private school by its actual group teaching, curriculum and educational aim. [Section 7 in the 2026 text](https://ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10009266&Paragraf=7&FassungVom=2026-10-18) requires notice to the competent school authority at least three months before intended opening. The [2026 amendment](https://www.bmb.gv.at/Themen/schule/schulrecht/gvo/psg_novelle.html) changes the process from **1 January 2027**; its future route is not presented as the current duty. Sites approaching that date must check the operative law and authority instructions.

[Schulordnung 2024 § 4](https://ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20012587&Paragraf=4&FassungVom=2026-10-18) requires a child-protection concept for covered schools. A private school's exact category and public status need checking with its school authority. The [special child and youth welfare criminal-record certificate](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/persoenliche_dokumente_und_bestaetigungen/strafregister/Seite.300020) is a role-specific route; a parent portal account alone is no reason to demand it. [Compulsory education guidance](https://www.bmb.gv.at/Themen/schule/schulsystem/sa/hu.html) distinguishes family pathways according to school status.

The core applies [EU GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) with [Austrian Data Protection Authority guidance](https://dsb.gv.at/rechte-pflichten/ihre-pflichten-als-verantwortlicher), [employer risk assessment](https://ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10008910&Paragraf=4&FassungVom=2026-10-18), [workplace first aid](https://ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10008910&Paragraf=26&FassungVom=2026-10-18), and [EU food hygiene](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32004R0852) where the site's activities trigger them. ICCE terms apply only to actual qualification participants.

Every Austrian sector/topic cell remains `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`, with regional status `IN_REVIEW`. [Regional inventory](regional-review.md) identifies all nine Länder. Kindergarten authorisation, staffing and safeguarding; building, fire and accessibility permits; youth supervision; local transport and specialist food approval are not complete. A site with no recorded Land cannot receive a presumed regional rule, and an unreviewed Land never means no obligation. Actual provider classification, funding and ACE/ICCE participation remain separate facts.

The pack's primary sources were checked against official legal/regulator pages. RIS links include a 2026 date parameter to avoid silently switching to the 2027 private-school text. See [PROGRESS.md](../../PROGRESS.md) for local checks, source access limits and PR evidence.
