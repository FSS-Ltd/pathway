# Austrian Länder inventory; duties still in review

Territorial inventory checked 21 October 2026 against the Austrian government's
[EDM list 3012](https://edm.gv.at/edm_portal/redaList.do?display=plain&lang=de&seqCode=yj86by532rpgb4).
Its nine ISO 3166-2 codes and German names are the source for catalogue 1.37.0
and the additive jurisdiction migration. The migration adds geography selectors,
not a reviewed Land-specific legal obligation. All 432 new Land/sector/topic
cells remain `IN_REVIEW`.

All nine Länder require separate validation before any regional coverage cell can be marked published. National actions are available; regional childcare, school administration, building/fire, youth-protection and local licensing details remain unknown until the site's Bundesland and provider category are confirmed.

The [Austrian public-service portal](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/bauen_und_wohnen/bauen/Seite.2260200) identifies nine building codes. Its [kindergarten overview](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/familie_und_partnerschaft/familie-und-kinderfuersorge/kinderbetreuung/2/Seite.370130) and [youth-supervision guidance](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/reisen_und_freizeit/vorschriften-fuer-jugendliche/1/Seite.1740302) point to Land-specific routes. For example, [Wiener Kindergartengesetz § 5](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=LrW&Gesetzesnummer=20000263&Paragraf=5) requires a kindergarten operating permit in Vienna, as the [Vienna authority's application page](https://www.wien.gv.at/amtswege/bewilligung-betrieb-kindergarten) also confirms; that rule is not applied to the other eight Länder. Validate the current law and authority for each Land before publishing an actionable regional item.

The [Austrian legal information system](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/UI/Erv/Info.aspx)
publishes distinct State Law Gazettes for all nine Länder and separate district
and municipal regulations. A selected Land does not resolve a district or
municipal permit, nor establish whether a site operates a kindergarten or
school. These lower-tier and provider-specific routes remain a separate review
boundary.
