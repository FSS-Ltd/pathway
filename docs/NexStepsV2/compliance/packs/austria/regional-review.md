# Austrian Länder still in review

National actions are available; regional childcare, school administration, building/fire, youth-protection and local licensing details remain unknown until the site's Bundesland and provider category are confirmed.

The [Austrian public-service portal](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/bauen_und_wohnen/bauen/Seite.2260200) identifies nine building codes. Its [kindergarten overview](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/familie_und_partnerschaft/familie-und-kinderfuersorge/kinderbetreuung/2/Seite.370130) and [youth-supervision guidance](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/reisen_und_freizeit/vorschriften-fuer-jugendliche/1/Seite.1740302) point to Land-specific routes. For example, [Wiener Kindergartengesetz § 5](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=LrW&Gesetzesnummer=20000263&Paragraf=5) requires a kindergarten operating permit in Vienna; that rule is not applied to the other eight Länder. Validate the current law and authority for each Land before publishing an actionable regional item.
