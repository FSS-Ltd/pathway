# Portugal institutional checklist, version 1.27.0

Reviewed 2026-10-20. This progressive pack publishes a national core across all six institutional sectors and eight topics, with distinct private-education authorisation actions for 18 mainland districts and the Azores and Madeira autonomous regions. The national 48 sector/topic cells are `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`. Regional registration cells are partial; other regional topics remain `IN_REVIEW`. Municipal permissions, sector-specific exceptions and site decisions remain incomplete. Manual confirmation measures checklist completion, not legal certification. The [CAOP2025 location review](caop-review.md) documents the 308 selectable municipalities.

The [mainland private/cooperative education statute](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/decreto-lei/2013-165096501) requires a school authorisation matched to the actual site and education level. The [Azores statute](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/decreto-legislativo-regional/2005-164920244) and [Madeira statute](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-legislativo-regional/15-2011-669862) have separate regional education routes. An ACE, Church or Learning Centre screen, or public financial support, does not itself establish legal school or public-provider status. Preschool and out-of-school care require classification from actual provision and operator. The [social-establishment law](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/decreto-lei/2007-115002318) has been amended to include communication routes and applies in autonomous regions with adaptations; confirm the current path rather than relying solely on a service page labelled as a licence. [Creche](https://www.gov.pt/servicos/creche-licenca-de-funcionamento) and [CATL](https://www.gov.pt/servicos/centro-de-atividades-de-tempos-livres-licenca-de-funcionamento) are separate social responses on the mainland.

[Child-protection law](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/lei/1999-34542475) requires response and referral decisions by actual entity and risk. [Criminal-record screening](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei/113-2009-490258) concerns recruitment to paid or unpaid functions with regular contact with minors, not adult portal accounts. The school [curriculum](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/55-2018-115652962) and [inclusive-education](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/54-2018-115652961) routes depend on recognised provision and regional implementation. [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), [Portuguese data law](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei/58-2019-123815982), [workplace safety](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei/102-2009-490009), [fire safety](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/220-2008-439866), food hygiene and [collective child transport](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/lei/2006-118874405) are conditional on actual controller, employer, premises or activity roles. The child-transport law was amended in 2026, so older guidance must not be treated as the only current authority. ICCE terms apply only to actual participants and do not confer Portuguese school recognition.

Each action records expected evidence, a source, applicability questions and a permitted N/A reason where appropriate. The user should check doubtful exclusions with the competent education, social-security, municipal, child-protection or activity authority. National publication does not remove the visible regional and local gaps; portal signals remain evidence and never tick a manual confirmation.
