# Portugal CAOP2025 district, region and municipality review

Reviewed 2026-10-20 against the [Direção-Geral do Território's CAOP2025 download](https://www.dgterritorio.gov.pt/atividades/cartografia/cartografia-tematica/caop?language=pt), approved on 18 February 2026. Its `Areas_Municipio_CAOP2025.csv` has 308 unique DTMN municipality codes: 278 mainland municipalities in 18 districts, 19 in the Azores and 11 in Madeira. The [Direção-Geral das Autarquias Locais](https://portalautarquico.dgal.gov.pt/pt-PT/administracao-local/entidades-autarquicas/municipios/) confirms the same split. The additive migration seeds 20 parent regions (`PT-D01`–`PT-D18`, `PT-AZO`, `PT-MAD`) and the 308 `PT-CAOP-` municipalities. It preserves official four-digit DTMN codes and island names in autonomous-region municipality labels. A source-to-disposable-database comparison matched all 308 codes, names and parent regions.

The district or autonomous-region parent identifies which education and social-service authority to consult. The selected municipality identifies the local site boundary, but does not establish a school authorisation, social-response permission, building use, fire decision, food registration or transport licence. Freguesias and island subdivisions are not separately selectable in this version; their applicable site conditions and municipal implementation remain in review.
