# Netherlands province and municipality review

Reviewed 2026-10-20 against [CBS's municipal classification effective 1 January 2026](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/onze-diensten/methoden/classificaties/overig/gemeentelijke-indelingen-per-jaar/indeling-per-jaar/gemeentelijke-indeling-op-1-januari-2026) and its linked [municipality workbook](https://www.cbs.nl/-/media/cbs/onze-diensten/methoden/classificaties/overig/gemeenten-alfabetisch-2026.xlsx). The additive migration seeds 12 provinces and 342 municipalities. `NL-PV##` and `NL-GM####` retain the official CBS province and municipality codes with a portal prefix. Each municipality has the province parent listed in the workbook.

This inventory supports exact site selection and keeps a missing municipality unresolved. It is not an inventory of municipal permits or a finding that the site has permission to operate. Municipality and GGD decisions on childcare and LRK, the current environmental plan and Bbl building use, fire-use notices, local safety measures, and conditions attached to education or activity approvals remain site-specific and in review. Review the actual address and provision with the municipality, GGD and other competent authority before treating an action as inapplicable.
