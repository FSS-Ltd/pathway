# Netherlands national institutional checklist, version 1.25.0

Reviewed 2026-10-20. This progressive pack publishes 29 conditional and classification actions with 36 official or provider sources across six institutional sectors and eight topics. All 48 Netherlands country cells remain `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`; province, municipality and site-specific legal review remains `IN_REVIEW`. Manual confirmation measures checklist completion, not legal certification. The [municipal review](municipal-review.md) describes the 12 provinces and 342 municipalities used for site selection.

Actual education status controls applicability. The [Education Inspectorate's private education categories](https://www.onderwijsinspectie.nl/onderwijssectoren/particulier-onderwijs/soorten-particulier-onderwijs) distinguish non-funded B3 schools, foreign or international B4 schools, examination institutions and other teaching. [DUO's B3 school process](https://duo.nl/zakelijk/voortgezet-onderwijs/stichten-en-opheffen/particuliere-school-oprichten.jsp) requires notification within four weeks for the covered route. A publicly funded denominational school is not a B3 school merely because it is privately governed; an ACE, Church or School screen proves neither status. Tuition centres must establish whether they provide regulated school, childcare or other teaching and check doubtful classification with the municipality, DUO or inspectorate.

[Municipal childcare registration and the LRK](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/kinderopvang/kinderopvang-starten) precede operation of covered childcare. [PRK and VOG duties](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/familie-zorg-en-gezondheid/kinderopvang/veiligheid-kinderopvang-verbeteren/personenregister-kinderopvang) follow actual work or structural presence, not ordinary parent portal access. A certificate's age for PRK entry is distinct from a universal certificate expiry. [Staffing ratios](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/familie-zorg-en-gezondheid/kinderopvang/vaste-gezichten-en-groepen-kinderopvang) depend on the service and ages. Covered education and childcare professionals need the [mandatory reporting code](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/recht-veiligheid-en-defensie/huiselijk-geweld/meldcode); covered schools have separate [social-safety duties](https://www.onderwijsinspectie.nl/onderwerpen/sociale-veiligheid/zorgplicht-veiligheid-leerlingen).

[GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), [Dutch AP guidance](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/nl/onderwerpen/onderwijs/gebruik-van-persoonsgegevens-het-onderwijs), [employer safety](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/arbeidsomstandigheden/waar-moet-mijn-werkgever-voor-zorgen-volgens-de-arbowet) and the current [Bbl building-use rules](https://iplo.nl/thema/toepassing-regels-praktijk/school/gebruiksregels/) apply according to the actual processing, employer and premises. [NVWA food registration](https://www.nvwa.nl/onderwerpen/voedselveiligheid/levensmiddelen-produceren-en-verhandelen/registratie-en-erkenning/registreer-uw-levensmiddelenbedrijf) has documented exceptions; food-safety controls may still apply. Transport licences depend on the operator, route and commercial status. ICCE provider duties apply only to actual participants and do not establish Dutch school status or qualification recognition.

The checklist leaves uncertain provider and municipal decisions visible. It asks for evidence, gives a reasoned N/A route only where permitted, and directs uncertain cases to the competent authority. Portal signals remain supporting evidence and never tick a manual confirmation. Publication at country level does not mean every province, municipality, building-use decision or individual licence condition has been reviewed.
