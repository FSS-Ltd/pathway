# Wales national pack — partial publication

Step 2.02 publishes 45 reviewed national actions backed by 51 source records across all six institutional research sectors and eight topics. Catalogue 1.3 retains England 1.2 history. All 48 Welsh sector/topic cells are `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`; their regional status is `IN_REVIEW`. A published action is a manual checklist item, not a finding of legal compliance. Missing provider classification, age, activity or controller facts leave applicability unresolved. Welsh requirements never fall back to England.

The current source observation and requirement effective date is 17 October 2026. Source review expires on 12 January 2027. Historical attestations remain separate from current requirements. See [regional review](regional-review.md) for the local coverage boundary.

## Source and applicability review

- [Welsh independent school registration guidance](https://www.gov.wales/independent-schools-registration-and-operational-guidance-html) distinguishes an actual school under the Education Act definition from a branded learning centre and explains registration before admitting pupils, returns and material changes. [2024 independent-school standards](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2024/27/schedule/data.xml) govern welfare, education and checks; the Wales-specific three-year check requirement is kept distinct from the general DBS statement that certificates have no official expiry.
- [Keeping learners safe](https://www.gov.wales/keeping-learners-safe) addresses Welsh schools and responsible bodies; the [community safeguarding code](https://www.gov.wales/working-together-safeguard-people-code-safeguarding-practice) is non-statutory. A parent account alone does not trigger a background check. Local reporting routes remain unverified.
- [Childcare registration exceptions](https://www.gov.wales/registering-childcare-provider-exceptions), [2026 childcare standards](https://www.gov.wales/national-minimum-standards-regulated-childcare), [Curriculum for Wales](https://www.gov.wales/curriculum-wales-statutory-guidance) and [ALN guidance](https://www.gov.wales/additional-learning-needs-aln-decision-making-and-communication-html) apply through actual registration, funding, age and school arrangements. Future April 2027 childcare exception changes are not treated as current.
- Current [UK GDPR](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/679/contents/data.xml), [Data Protection Act 2018](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12/contents/data.xml) and [ICO fee guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/data-protection-fee/exemptions/) support controller duties and conditional assessment of DPO, DPIA, special-category, records and fees. Privacy complaints and rights timing use their documented 2026 commencement dates. Processing role and actual activities must be established before a conditional duty is marked applicable.
- [HSE employer risk and first aid](https://www.hse.gov.uk/simple-health-safety/), [fire responsibility](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1541/article/9/data.xml), [Equality Act accessibility planning](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/15/schedule/10/data.xml), [assimilated food-hygiene registration and HACCP duties](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/852/contents/data.xml) and [FSA food registration](https://register.food.gov.uk/new?lang=en), [Welsh food rating](https://www.gov.wales/food-hygiene-rating-wales-act-2013) and [CIW residential classifications](https://www.careinspectorate.wales/regulations-statutory-guidance-and-national-minimum-standards-adult-and-childrens-services) support bounded operational actions. Shared premises, statutory exceptions, vehicle licensing and actual rated/regulated-service scope require site-specific assessment.
- [ICCE registration](https://www.icce.education/school-hea-registration), [training](https://www.icce.education/training), [enrolment](https://www.icce.education/enrolling-students) and [moderation](https://www.icce.education/moderation-1) apply only to participating institutions. ACE-only contract terms and regional approvals require the current external agreement; portal signals do not grant approval.

The runtime source records contain authority, provision, classification, territorial scope, effective/observed dates, verification date and review expiry. Each action records the trigger, expected evidence and unresolved-fact explanation. Local and specialist branches remain visible gaps, including safeguarding-board procedures, local permissions, specialist school/childcare/residential routes, activity licences and institution-specific contracts.
