# Wales regional review boundary

Observed 17 October 2026. Authority discovery is reviewed; individual local
requirements and service contacts remain **IN_REVIEW**, not reviewed absence.
The runtime pack requires region/local-authority information but does not claim
that entering a place name completes a local review.

[Welsh Government planning authority directory](https://www.gov.wales/find-your-local-planning-authority)
identifies 22 council planning areas and three national-park authorities. The
responsible planning body can differ from the council responsible for children’s
services. Council inventory: Blaenau Gwent, Bridgend, Caerphilly, Cardiff,
Carmarthenshire, Ceredigion, Conwy, Denbighshire, Flintshire, Gwynedd, Isle of
Anglesey, Merthyr Tydfil, Monmouthshire, Neath Port Talbot, Newport, Pembrokeshire,
Powys, Rhondda Cynon Taf, Swansea, Torfaen, Vale of Glamorgan and Wrexham.
Park inventory: Bannau Brycheiniog, Pembrokeshire Coast and Eryri.

[Welsh Government safeguarding review directory](https://www.gov.wales/single-unified-safeguarding-review-guidance)
identifies six safeguarding-board areas. National identification does not verify a
site’s current referral route, designated officer, emergency contact or training
terms. Obtain those directly from the responsible board/council before confirming
the site’s procedures.

## Outstanding site-specific review

For every institutional sector and topic, establish actual premises/provision and
then review the responsible authority’s current terms. This includes planning/use
and building-control permissions, fire responsibility and shared-premises
allocation, food authority registration/rating, safeguarding referral and staff
allegation procedures, commissioned/funded provision, admission/attendance and
ALN arrangements, transport/trip licensing and residential care classification.
Charity, denomination, sporting body, ACE/provider and commissioner conditions
are separate evidence questions. Do not infer them from institutional branding.

No council or park-specific checklist duty is published in this release. All 48
Welsh sector/topic cells remain PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED with IN_REVIEW regional status.
An unknown region or a locally unreviewed duty stays a coverage gap. A national
manual confirmation cannot establish local approval or legal certification.
