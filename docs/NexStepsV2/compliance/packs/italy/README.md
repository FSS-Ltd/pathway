# Italy national checklist pack (2.19)

Reviewed 2026-10-20. Catalogue 1.20.0 adds **23 conditional actions** and **19 official/provider sources** across six institutional sectors and eight topics. All 48 Italy country cells are `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`, with regional status `IN_REVIEW`. The manual completion count is not legal certification.

Actual provision controls the route. [Law 62/2000](https://www.normattiva.it/atto/caricaDettaglioAtto?atto.codiceRedazionale=000G0099&atto.dataPubblicazioneGazzetta=2000-03-21) separates state schools and recognised _paritarie_ from other non-state schools. [Ministerial Decree 263/2007](https://www.mim.gov.it/documents/20182/0/Decreto%2Bministeriale%2B263%2Bdel%2B29%2Bnovembre%2B2007%2BRegolamento%2Brecante%2B%27%27Disciplina%2Bdelle%2Bmodalit%C3%A0%2Bprocedimentali%2Bper%2Bl%27inclusione%2Bed%2Bil%2Bmantenimento%2Bnell%27elenco%2Bregionale%2Bdelle%2Bscuole%2Bnon%2Bparitarie%27%27.pdf/1dae9996-82b8-4e53-a45e-c21608d80d2f?t=1500479396094&version=1.0) defines the regional list route for non-parity schools. [Legislative Decree 65/2017](https://www.normattiva.it/atto/caricaDettaglioAtto?atto.codiceRedazionale=17G00073&atto.dataPubblicazioneGazzetta=2017-05-16) establishes birth-to-six services with regional, autonomous-province and municipal implementation. The [INAPP accreditation portal](https://www.inapp.gov.it/accreditamento/) locates distinct territorial vocational-training routes. A Nexsteps vertical cannot establish any of these legal classifications or public funding.

The [Justice Ministry](https://www.giustizia.it/giustizia/page/it/come_fare_per_certificati_casellario_datore_lavoro?tab=f) describes the child-contact certificate as an employer hiring check for defined contractual roles with direct and regular contact, not a blanket requirement for adult portal accounts or an automatic six-month repeat. School bullying duties draw on [Law 70/2024](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/id/2024/05/30/24G00086/SG). [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) and the [Garante's school guidance](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/web/guest/home/docweb/-/docweb-display/docweb/10190259) inform privacy actions. ICCE terms apply only where those qualifications are actually offered, separately from Italian school permission.

The [regional review](regional-review.md) records the twenty regions and two autonomous provinces. School-office decisions, regional early-years and vocational rules, municipal permissions, fire/health conditions, transport and residential activities remain in review. No checkbox is ticked automatically.
