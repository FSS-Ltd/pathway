# Italy region, autonomous-province and municipal review

Reviewed 2026-10-20. [Istat](https://www.istat.it/classificazione/codici-dei-comuni-delle-province-e-delle-regioni/) confirms twenty regions; the [ISO 3166-2 list](https://www.iso.org/iso/iso_3166-2_newsletter_ii-1_corrected_2010-02-19.pdf) supplies their `IT-` codes and the `IT-TN`/`IT-BZ` codes for autonomous Trento and Bolzano. The migration makes twenty regions and the two autonomous provinces selectable at the same location level. The older ISO notice is used for stable codes, not as a current inventory of every province; Istat is the current regional cross-check.

The regions are Abruzzo (`IT-65`), Basilicata (`IT-77`), Calabria (`IT-78`), Campania (`IT-72`), Emilia-Romagna (`IT-45`), Friuli-Venezia Giulia (`IT-36`), Lazio (`IT-62`), Liguria (`IT-42`), Lombardia (`IT-25`), Marche (`IT-57`), Molise (`IT-67`), Piemonte (`IT-21`), Puglia (`IT-75`), Sardegna (`IT-88`), Sicilia (`IT-82`), Toscana (`IT-52`), Trentino-Alto Adige/Südtirol (`IT-32`), Umbria (`IT-55`), Valle d'Aosta/Vallée d'Aoste (`IT-23`) and Veneto (`IT-34`). Trento (`IT-TN`) and Bolzano/Bozen (`IT-BZ`) require separate autonomous-province review.

The current pack publishes national entry routes and asks for the operating region or autonomous province when a territorial decision is needed. It does not publish each USR decision, regional early-years or vocational-accreditation schedule, or municipality's building, care, food and camp conditions. All sector/topic country cells remain partial and regional review remains `IN_REVIEW`.
