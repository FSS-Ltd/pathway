# Slovakia institutional checklist, version 1.29.0

This is a **partial** national and kraj release for all six institutional sectors and eight tracker topics. It is a manual planning checklist, not legal certification. Actual school registration, childcare route, staff role, activities, operator and site decide applicability. An ACE or Public Learning Centre screen is not evidence of state funding or school status.

## Reviewed entry points

| Topic              | Source and applicability decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Registration       | [Law 321/2025](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2025/321/20260101.print.html) uses a school and facility register from 1 January 2026. Its public-provider route is separate from private and church school registration, with transitional school-year timing. [Law 448/2008](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2008/448/20260701.print.html) describes a distinct social-service route for some under-three care. Provider classification and authority decisions remain site-specific. |
| Safeguarding       | [Law 305/2005 § 7](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2005/305/20260101.print.html) requires notification of child-rights violations to the child social protection authority. Restricted case details remain in safeguarding, not generic tracker evidence.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Personnel          | [Law 138/2019 §§ 15–15a](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2019/138/20260901.print.html) concerns covered pedagogical and professional staff. The 2026 child-and-youth criminal-register extract is requested through the regional office, which communicates the eligibility decision to the employer; it does not share the extract content. Foreign-national document and translation rules differ. This route does not attach to every adult portal account.                                 |
| Privacy            | [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) and [Law 18/2018](https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2018/18/) require controller-specific processing controls. DPIA and DPO triggers need an actual assessment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Premises           | [Law 124/2006](https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2006/124/20250701) addresses employer safety; [Law 314/2001](https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2001/314/) and [Law 355/2007](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2007/355/20260801.print.html) require fire and child-facility health review against actual premises and use.                                                                                                                           |
| Education and care | The [School Act 245/2008](https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2008/245/20260901) governs covered schools and preschool education. Under-three social care has a different [Law 448/2008](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2008/448/20260701.print.html) route. A nursery-style screen proves neither.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Activities         | Actual food business activity follows [Regulation 852/2004](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2004/852/oj/eng) and Slovak [public-health law](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2007/355/20260801.print.html). A day trip does not automatically meet the separate residential-event definition; duration, group size and ages must be checked.                                                                                                                                                  |
| ACE/ICCE           | [ICCE registration](https://www.icce.education/school-hea-registration), [enrolment](https://www.icce.education/enrolling-students) and [moderation](https://www.icce.education/moderation-1) apply only where ICCE qualifications are actually offered. They do not establish Slovak school or qualification recognition.                                                                                                                                                                             |

## Territory and remaining review

The additive migration seeds eight kraj selectors from [Eurostat NUTS 2024](https://gisco-services.ec.europa.eu/distribution/v2/nuts/csv/NUTS_AT_2024.csv). These are territorial identifiers, not permissions. The [Slovak Statistical Office territorial register](https://slovak.statistics.sk/wps/portal/ext/Databases/REGPJ/) identifies municipality and district relationships, but its ZIP download returned HTTP 403 during this review. Municipality selectors were therefore not seeded from unverified third-party copies. All national cells are `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`; only regional registration cells contain a reviewed action, while remaining regional cells stay `IN_REVIEW`.

For full coverage, review current municipality and district records, regional school-office and public-health decisions, non-pedagogical child-facing roles, school and preschool programme details, childcare staffing and capacities, accessible premises, food registration, trips, transport, boarding and camp/event permissions, plus church and charity legal-form duties. Confirm uncertain exceptions with the competent regional or local authority before recording N/A. The final programme-wide audit must recheck all current source versions and conditional rules.
