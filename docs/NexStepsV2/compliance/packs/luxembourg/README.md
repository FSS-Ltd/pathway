# Luxembourg national institutional checklist, version 1.23.0

Reviewed 2026-10-20. This progressive national pack publishes 27 conditional actions with 30 official or provider sources across all six institutional sectors and eight topics. Every Luxembourg country cell remains `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`; canton, commune and site-specific review remains `IN_REVIEW`. Manual confirmation measures checklist completion, not legal certification.

The [private-school law](https://legilux.public.lu/eli/etat/leg/loi/2003/06/13/n1/jo) and [Education Ministry school-offer guidance](https://men.public.lu/fr/systeme-educatif/fondamental/offre-scolaire-organisation/offre-scolaire.html) distinguish authorised private schools from public schools and other provision. The [SEA approval guidance](https://guichet.public.lu/fr/entreprises/creation-developpement/profession/profession-encadrement/creche-foyer-agrement.html) separately covers qualifying paid childcare, its ministry approval, premises and operating conditions. Its classification depends on actual children, payment, capacity and activity. An ACE or Learning Centre screen cannot establish school authorisation, public funding or an SEA exemption; the site must verify uncertain classifications with MENJE and its commune. [Mini-crèches](https://guichet.public.lu/fr/entreprises/sectoriel/services-accueil/mini-creches/mini-creche-agrement.html) have a distinct route.

The [child-protection referral guidance](https://men.public.lu/fr/systeme-educatif/droits-enfant/protection-violence/signalement.html) informs safeguarding arrangements appropriate to the institution. [Bulletin n° 5 guidance](https://guichet.public.lu/fr/citoyens/justice/certificats-copies-actes-casier-judiciaire/extrait-casier-judiciaire/casier-judiciaire-pers-physique.html) applies to professional or volunteer regular child-contact roles, not every adult portal user. An SEA can have broader role and recency conditions under its approval; a recently presented extract is not a universally expiring certificate. [CNPD guidance](https://cnpd.public.lu/fr/professionnels/obligations/obligations-rgpd.html) and GDPR govern actual processing; DPIA and DPO decisions remain conditional.

Employer safety, premises, food, transport and trip actions depend on the actual operator and activity. [ALVA's food notification guidance](https://guichet.public.lu/fr/entreprises/sectoriel/alimentation/securite-alimentaire/enregistrement.html) applies to qualifying food activity. [Adult learning guidance](https://men.public.lu/fr/systeme-educatif/formation-adultes/cours-interet-general.html) must not be treated as automatic programme approval for tutoring, and [nationality-course approval](https://men.public.lu/fr/systeme-educatif/formation-adultes/integration-nationalite/cours-examen.html) is limited to that offered route. ICCE conditions apply only to actual participating institutions and do not establish Luxembourg recognition.

The [communal review](communal-review.md) records the current 12 cantons and 100 communes. The pack does not claim a commune-level legal review; families' school-admission routes and individual site decisions must be confirmed with the competent authority. Missing commune information remains unresolved and portal signals never complete a checkbox.
