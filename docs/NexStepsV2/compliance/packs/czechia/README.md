# Czechia national checklist pack (2.11)

Reviewed 2026-10-19. Catalogue 1.12.0 adds **15 conditional actions** and **18 official/provider sources** across six institutional sectors and eight topics. All 48 Czechia cells remain `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED` with regional status `IN_REVIEW`. Manual checklist completion is not legal certification.

[The public administration portal](https://portal.gov.cz/en/sluzby-vs/registration-in-the-register-of-schools-and-school-facilities-by-the-regional-authority-S1305) explains the school-register effect and regional registration route. [The ministry's founder guidance](https://msmt.gov.cz/vzdelavani/skolsky-rejstrik/informace-o-postupu-pri-zalozeni-nove-skoly) separates forming an entity from registering school activity and distinguishes public from non-public founders. [The Labour Office](https://up.gov.cz/pro-poskytovatele-1) administers ordinary and neighbourhood children's-group authorisation through a separate provider register. The pack asks sites to classify actual provision. ACE or Learning Centre screens do not establish school status, government funding, or a children's-group subtype.

[Ministry prevention materials](https://msmt.gov.cz/vzdelavani/specialni-vzdelavani-prevence-institucionalni-vychova/prevence) support a child-protection referral procedure, while [pedagogical qualifications](https://msmt.gov.cz/vzdelavani/pedagogicti-pracovnici/odborna-kvalifikace-pedagogickeho-pracovnika) and [children's-group carer conditions](https://up.gov.cz/pro-poskytovatele-1) depend on actual roles. [EU GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) and [Czech regulator guidance](https://uoou.gov.cz/index.php/verejnost/zakladni-prirucka-k-ochrane-udaju) depend on processing roles. [Labour-inspectorate guidance](https://suip.gov.cz/a3d-bezpecnost-a-ochrana-zdravi-pri-praci-bozp) addresses employer safety. [Food authority guidance](https://www.szpi.gov.cz/podnikatele.aspx) and EU hygiene law apply to actual food-business activity. ICCE terms apply only where qualifications are offered.

The [regional inventory](regional-review.md) records 14 regions and unresolved local rules. School categories, care ratios and inspection, suitability checks, premises, food subtypes and transport require further authority review. Site managers should confirm uncertain applicability with the competent authority before marking a review action not applicable. No automatic checkbox completion, UI change or database migration is introduced.
