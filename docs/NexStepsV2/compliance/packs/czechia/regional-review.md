# Czechia regional and municipal rules still in review

The [Czech Statistical Office](https://csu.gov.cz/kraje-spolecne/uzemni-jednotky) lists 14 regions (`kraje`), including Prague. Regional school-register authorities and local premises, hygiene and food authorities can change the route for a particular site. No regional checklist is published in this step; `IN_REVIEW` is not a finding of no obligations.

| Boundary                                | Reviewed authority signpost                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Still required before complete local coverage                                                         |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fourteen regional education authorities | [Regional school-register service](https://portal.gov.cz/en/sluzby-vs/registration-in-the-register-of-schools-and-school-facilities-by-the-regional-authority-S1305)                                                               | Verify each region's school types, applications, changes, inspections and local contacts.             |
| Ministry education route                | [Separate ministry-register service](https://portal.gov.cz/en/sluzby-vs/application-for-registration-in-the-register-of-schools-and-educational-establishments-by-the-ministry-of-education-youth-and-sports-S13097)               | Identify founder and school categories registered by the ministry rather than a region.               |
| Children's groups                       | [Labour Office provider guidance](https://up.gov.cz/pro-poskytovatele-1)                                                                                                                                                           | Confirm ordinary or neighbourhood group status, local contact, capacity, staff and premises evidence. |
| Municipal and health authorities        | [School-register service](https://portal.gov.cz/en/sluzby-vs/registration-in-the-register-of-schools-and-school-facilities-by-the-regional-authority-S1305) and [children's-group guidance](https://up.gov.cz/pro-poskytovatele-1) | Verify building use, fire and hygiene approvals for actual premises and activities.                   |
| Food safety                             | [Food inspection authority](https://www.szpi.gov.cz/podnikatele.aspx)                                                                                                                                                              | Confirm catering subtype, competent authority and any specialist approval.                            |

The operating region and municipality must be captured from the site, not inferred from the organisation billing address or browser timezone. The 14-region inventory is a review boundary, not 14 published regional packs.
