# Cyprus national checklist pack (2.10)

Reviewed 2026-10-19. Catalogue 1.11.0 adds **15 conditional actions** and **15 official/provider sources** across six institutional sectors and eight topics. All 48 Cyprus cells remain `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED` with regional status `IN_REVIEW`. Manual checklist completion is not legal certification.

The [Ministry of Education's private-education guidance](https://www.moec.gov.cy/en/private_education.html) separates private schools from ministry-approved private institutes. [Pre-school care](https://www.businessincyprus.gov.cy/business-sectors/day-care-centre-for-pre-school-children/) and [school-age day care](https://www.businessincyprus.gov.cy/business-sectors/day-care-centre-for-school-age-children/) use separate Social Welfare registration routes. The pack asks each site to classify actual provision. An ACE or Learning Centre screen set does not establish legal school status or public funding. Out-of-school teaching receives an institute-route review; childcare receives a day-care-route review, because these profile flags alone do not establish the legal subtype.

The [Cyprus Police safeguarding function](https://www.gov.cy/police/en/departments/crime-combating-department/safeguarding-sub-directorate/) supports a referral route. Official day-care guidance lists role-specific clearances and qualifications, but an adult portal account alone is not a staff role. The [Cyprus labour inspectorate](https://www.mlsi.gov.cy/mlsi/dli/dliup.nsf/73B69511D2914599C2257E27002BB55E/%24file/ASFALEIA%20_YGEIA_STHN_ERGASIA%20en.pdf) requires written workplace risk assessment. [EU GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) and the [Cyprus data protection commissioner's guidance](https://www.dataprotection.gov.cy/dataprotection/dataprotection.nsf/ED1AAB60C3F3A93BC22582600037E15D/%24file/Guide%20-%20Record%20of%20activities%20ENG.pdf) depend on processing roles. [Food-establishment registration](https://www.gov.cy/moh-mphs-phs/documents/katachorisi-egkatastaseon-epicheiriseon-trofimon/) and EU hygiene law apply only to actual food-business activity. ICCE requirements apply only to qualification providers.

The [district inventory](regional-review.md) lists unresolved local rules. School and institute approval details, child-care ratios and inspection, role-specific checks, premises, transport and specialist food routes need further authority review. Site managers should confirm uncertain applicability with the competent local authority before marking a review action not applicable. No automatic checkbox completion, UI change or database migration is introduced.
