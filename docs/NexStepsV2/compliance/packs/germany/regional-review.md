# Germany Länder and local-authority review

Reviewed 2026-10-20. The [Federal Agency for Cartography and Geodesy's Länder inventory](https://sgx.geodatenzentrum.de/web_public/gdz/dokumentation/deu/annex_vg.pdf) lists all 16 Länder and their ISO 3166-2 suffixes. The additive migration makes Baden-Württemberg (`DE-BW`), Bayern (`DE-BY`), Berlin (`DE-BE`), Brandenburg (`DE-BB`), Bremen (`DE-HB`), Hamburg (`DE-HH`), Hessen (`DE-HE`), Mecklenburg-Vorpommern (`DE-MV`), Niedersachsen (`DE-NI`), Nordrhein-Westfalen (`DE-NW`), Rheinland-Pfalz (`DE-RP`), Saarland (`DE-SL`), Sachsen (`DE-SN`), Sachsen-Anhalt (`DE-ST`), Schleswig-Holstein (`DE-SH`) and Thüringen (`DE-TH`) selectable under Germany.

[KMK's 2026 state-law inventory](https://www.kmk.org/en/downloads-dokumente/rechtsvorschriften-/-lehrplaene/uebersicht-schulgesetze.html) is the starting point for school legislation. This pack does not publish sixteen interchangeable school approvals or claim to settle curricula, teacher qualifications, inspection, daycare staffing ratios or Land funding. Local building, fire, health, food, transport and youth-welfare decisions also remain in review. The national actions identify the route and require local confirmation where provision is uncertain. All sector/topic country cells remain partial and regional status remains `IN_REVIEW`; a country-level action never means these duties are complete.
