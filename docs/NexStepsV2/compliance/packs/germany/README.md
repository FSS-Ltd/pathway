# Germany national checklist pack (2.16)

Reviewed 2026-10-20. Catalogue 1.17.0 adds **18 conditional actions** and **21 official/provider sources** across six institutional sectors and eight topics. All 48 Germany country cells are `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`, with regional status `IN_REVIEW`. A manual completion count is not legal certification.

Actual provision, rather than a Nexsteps screen or ACE label, determines school and childcare status. [KMK's private-school guidance](https://www.kmk.org/en/bildungsministerkonferenz/vertiefende-bildungsinhalte/allgemeinbildende-schulen/schulen-in-freier-traegerschaft.html) distinguishes state-approved _Ersatzschulen_ and points to each Land's school laws. The [current law inventory](https://www.kmk.org/en/downloads-dokumente/rechtsvorschriften-/-lehrplaene/uebersicht-schulgesetze.html) is an entry point, not a substitute for the operating Land's authority decision. Missing Land information leaves private-school actions unresolved.

For youth-welfare establishments, [SGB VIII section 45](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_8/__45.html) defines a permit route and express exceptions. [Section 47](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_8/__47.html) covers notifications and records for permit-liable establishments. [Sections 8a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_8/__8a.html) and [72a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_8/__72a.html) inform protection and role-specific checks; a parent or ordinary adult portal account alone is not a reason to demand a certificate. [IfSG sections 33](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ifsg/__33.html)–[36](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ifsg/__36.html) apply to qualifying community facilities and include infection and hygiene routes. GDPR, federal employment-data law, employer risk assessment and actual food service add conditional operational actions. ICCE terms apply only where qualifications are offered.

The [regional review](regional-review.md) records all sixteen Länder and the state-law work still open. Managers should retain authority decisions and check uncertain exclusions with the Land school authority, Landesjugendamt, Jugendamt or Gesundheitsamt before marking an action not applicable. No checkbox is ticked automatically.
