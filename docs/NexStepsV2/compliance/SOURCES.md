Country-pack work must expand these seeds for all sectors/topics, identify the
national and relevant regional legislation/regulators, and add precise provisions,
effective dates, evidence expectations and review metadata. Do not extrapolate
England's requirements across the UK or a common EU regulation across every
national operational requirement. Record inaccessible sources as research gaps.
